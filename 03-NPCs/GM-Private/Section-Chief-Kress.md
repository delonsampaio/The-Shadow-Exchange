# Section Chief Dorian Kress

**Look** — Never in the room. Operates through budget line items, routing decisions, and manufactured contracts.
**Mannerism** — His name appears at the bottom of forms the crew has already signed. They will notice it eventually.
**Drive** — The Twice-Born are a contamination to be eliminated. The licensed firm system is a mistake Apex Aegis will eventually correct.

## Role

Kress does not know the crew exists yet. He will notice them when they do something he considers a problem — protecting a Twice-Born, refusing a termination contract, or interfering with an Apex operation.

When he notices them, it will be through the paper trail. A contract will be rerouted. A compliance review will be scheduled. A competing firm will be given their next job instead. He does not confront directly. He manages.

## The Kress-Apex Connection

Kress and Apex Aegis want compatible things for different reasons. Apex wants monopoly. Kress wants elimination. They cooperate on contracts that serve both. Neither party would admit to the arrangement.

**The reveal:** Mid-game. After the crew has been frustrated by manufactured bureaucratic obstacles, they find Kress's name on the originating form for a particularly bad job. He is the reason Vantage & Lowe's last job went the way it did. He does not know the crew has found this.

## What Makes Him Dangerous

He has access to the Ministry's full routing infrastructure. He can isolate the crew from favorable assignments, manufacture compliance flags, and make their license conditional. He has never used violence directly. He has never needed to.

## Dialogue Voice

The crew will not speak to Kress directly for a long time. When they do:

- *On the Twice-Born:* "They are classified biological anomalies. The sentiment is irrelevant to the classification."
- *On the crew's record:* "Incomplete extractions cost more than they save. I've noted this in your file."
- *When the crew threatens to expose the Apex connection:* "That would require documentation. I'd suggest you think carefully about whether you have it." Pause. "Do you?"
