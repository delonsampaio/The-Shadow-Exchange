# The Whisperer

**Look** — Unremarkable in a deliberate way. No detail that would help you describe them later.
**Mannerism** — Makes eye contact exactly as long as necessary. Asks a clarifying question before answering anything.
**Drive** — Information wants to move. They facilitate.

## What They Offer

- Ministry personnel movements and rotation schedules
- Apex Aegis acquisition lists and active operation targets
- Which Ministry bounties are manufactured (Apex-influenced)
- Subway community intelligence — who is nervous, who has moved, who is new

## Price

Trades information for information. Bring something new. The quality of what you get matches the quality of what you bring. They do not accept Coin or Ethereal Credits.

**GM note:** The crew needs to gather intelligence during scores to have anything worth trading here. This incentivizes thorough investigation over fast extraction.

## Dialogue Voice

- *On a trade:* "Is that a question or are you telling me you don't know yet?"
- *On a weak offer:* "You already know that. So do I. Try again."
- *On Apex Aegis:* "They have a list. Your name isn't on it yet. The word 'yet' is doing work in that sentence."
- *On the Mimic:* A pause. "That's a better question than you know you're asking." Gives a partial answer. Keeps the rest.

## Notes

The Whisperer's booth is not consistently in the same location between visits. The crew will need to find it each time — which is itself information about whether the Whisperer wants to be found.
