# Gerald — The Bulldog With Human Hands

**Look** — A fawn English bulldog, nine years old, sitting very upright. **Two human hands where his front paws should be** — pale, careful, slightly wrinkled, with a faint tan line where a watch might sit.
**Mannerism** — Treats everyone as a long-lost friend. Offers handshakes. Claps when pleased. Seems to know that the hands are unusual and has decided to be gracious about it.
**Drive** — Be near whoever is kind to him. Open the door. Everyone should be inside, together.

## How he got the hands
**GM-private:** Merton's failing collar has been *bleeding Resonance* into the basement where Gerald sleeps three nights a week. A bulldog is not a Barghest — but it is a very good conductor. The hands are the first visible leak.

## What he does *(🎭 use freely — Comedy Playbook)*
- Shakes hands. Tries to shake the *Ministry's* hand. Gets confused when the Ministry doesn't offer one back.
- Opens doors. Cannot be stopped from opening doors. Cannot close them behind him.
- Covers his eyes with his hands if anyone raises a weapon.
- Thumbs-up that lasts too long.
- **Refuses to go near Elias Thorne.** Hides behind the nearest PC and whines. (Dogs know. Tell for the Understudied; also an Omen echo.)

## As a Cohort *(proposed — if the table keeps him)*
- **Type:** Expert (a "door-opener / witness-detector").
- **Edges:** Opens anything; knows when someone is lying (barks).
- **Flaws:** Cannot lie about what he saw; scene-stealer; one human-handed thumbs-up away from exposing the cover-up.
- **Harm / Notes:** The hands are **permanent** once manifested (mending the collar only stops *further* bleed). If kept, he rides the van (see `Comedy-Playbook.md`) and is **brave exactly once** — spend it well.
