# Renwick Stalls — Property Developer

**Look** — A nervous man in an expensive coat that is a size too big, as if bought for a better version of him. Smile that arrives a half-second early.
**Mannerism** — Says **"investment opportunity"** in every conversation, at least three times. Hands out business cards with **two phone numbers; neither is his.**
**Drive** — Clear the Ashby property before the end of the month so that Stalls Holdings can sell the parcel to Apex for a very large number.
**Secret** — He doesn't know what Apex does. He knows it pays well. He knows it will pay *less* if he fails.

## What he's really doing
Stalls Holdings is an Apex shell that buys property where a **Resonance anomaly** is "hindering development," has the Ministry clear the anomaly (via a legitimate-looking Form 88-D), and flips the cleared parcel to Apex. The **two extra pages** on his version of Form 88-D are an assignment of proceeds.

## 🎭 The Scooby Villain
**Timeline:** S3 phone-only contract (Form 88-D, two extra pages) → **S4** unmasking and tantrum (he is a patsy, not the top) → S5–6 he is pressed or hides → **S7** *The Shell Game*: his binder carries Kress's letterhead and the C&C appraisal marked "LEASEHOLDER UNVERIFIABLE — HOLD"; the crew chooses his fate. When the crew confronts him (Session 4), play it as a classic unmasking: he panics, throws a (small, ineffective) tantrum, and says some version of *"I would have gotten away with it if it weren't for you meddling hunters!"* — and **means it sincerely.** Then, quieter: he's terrified of the people above him. That gives the crew a choice.

## Voice
- "It's a *wonderful* opportunity."
- "I'm sure you're very professional. I'm also sure my investor is *extremely* professional."
- "I'm not saying *clear it today.* I'm saying clear it **soon.** For the opportunity."
- *When caught:* "It's not what it looks like! It's — it's a *holding structure!*"

## Hooks
- He has *seen a letterhead* from "the investor's people at the Ministry." **He never says the name Kress on screen** (the name appears at S7, in his binder). He does not know what it means.
- Hands them Apex's first hint about the bounty-skimming pipeline (Session 4).
