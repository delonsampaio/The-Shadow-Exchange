# Deputy Director Alma Reyes

**Look** — Mid-fifties. Reads the crew's reports with a pen in hand. The pen doesn't always move.
**Mannerism** — Gives the crew more time than the standard allows — never says this aloud.
**Drive** — The current system creates more exposure risk than it prevents. She is changing it slowly, from inside.

## Role

The Oversight Desk routes the crew's contracts. The crew gets slightly better terms — a day more, a clearer brief, a warning when a job has been flagged as complicated — because Reyes decides who gets what. She has never acknowledged this.

## Agenda

She believes licensed firms with genuine ethics are the cover-up's best asset. The extraction-only model creates bodies, grieving relatives, and inconsistencies that the standard explanations can't cover. She is not sentimental — she is calculating that the crew's approach has a better risk profile.

## What She Would Do

If the crew brings her documented evidence of Apex Aegis corruption — specifically, the Kress-Apex connection — she will act. Carefully. Slowly. In a way that protects her career first. The outcome will be real. The timeline will be Ministry-slow.

## Relationship Trigger

She will not acknowledge the favorable routing unless directly and privately confronted with proof that the crew has noticed. If they do: "I don't know what you're referring to. Is there something specific you needed?"

## Dialogue Voice

- *On a routing decision:* "Your next assignment has some unusual parameters. I've noted them in the brief."
- *On Apex Aegis:* "They produce results. The question is always what the results cost."
- *When the crew approaches her with Kress evidence:* Very still. Sets the pen down. "Close the door."
- *On the Twice-Born:* "Registration and management is a solvable problem. Nobody wants to solve it."
