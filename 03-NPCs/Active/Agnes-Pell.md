# Agnes Pell — The Neighbor

**Look** — Sixties, a quilted coat in a color that doesn't exist in nature, a very large tureen carried in two hands like a sacrament, her own ladle in her pocket.
**Mannerism** — Takes charge of any room she enters. Says "*Now.*" before every instruction. Refers to the dead as "the ones who've gone ahead."
**Drive** — To see her friend Mabel — as she has every Thursday for forty years.
**Secret (open question)** — She has seen a *living* Mabel for four decades. Whether she ever suspected otherwise is the quiet question under the comedy.

## 🎭 The Brass Lantern Irregulars *(her running gag)*
Agnes follows a weekly penny-dreadful serial about "monster hunters," and treats it as news. She will **quote it, wrongly and at length,** and tell the crew who *they* are in it (always the wrong PC for each role). New installment each session; the details drift further from the truth — until one day, **one detail is right**, and no one told her.

## Voice
- "Now. Who's holding the ladle?"
- "I read about you lot. Moonlight swords. I said to Mabel, *I'll believe it when someone invites me for tea.*"
- *To the PC she's decided is "the jaw":* "You've got a *lovely* jaw. I'd have said so in the serial."
- *If Mabel is gone:* "You look peaky." *(Quiet. Looking past them.)* "She wasn't well, was she."

## What she needs
Not to be lied to *and* not to be hurt. The crew's choice about Mabel decides which she gets.
