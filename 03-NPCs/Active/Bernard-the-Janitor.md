# Bernard — The Janitor

**Look** — Late fifties, cardigan under a gray work shirt, a mop he holds like a rifle. Fifteen years at the theater. A man who has seen *things* and filed all of them under "not my department."
**Mannerism** — Looks at an impossible thing for a long second, says "Okay," then **screams a very small scream.**
**Drive** — To finish the night, mop the lobby, and go home to a quiet apartment and a quiet cat.

## The Gag *(🟢 overt)*
- First sighting of anything strange: a very small scream; sprints to the supply closet; muffled **"I'M FINE. I'M NOT HERE."** A mop falls out.
- Ten seconds later he emerges, with full dignity, with a full bucket: *"I will be on Level Two."*
- Every appearance: he goes to **a higher level** of the building. By the third appearance he is on the roof.

## Mechanics
A *witness* if handled badly (Detection +1). A **free alibi** if handled well — he will swear anything to anyone *as long as no one asks him to look at it again.* If the crew treated Gerald kindly, Bernard remembers.

## Callback
When the Subway Decay hits 4 and the Fairground starts to flicker, Bernard is on the *roof of the theater,* wrapped in a blanket, watching the sky with an expression of enormous calm.
