# Director Arthur Vance

**Look** — A man who has worn the same expression for twenty years. Pressed everything. Reads files while people are still talking.
**Mannerism** — Refers to the crew's firm by its registration number, not its name. Corrects himself once, then goes back to the number.
**Drive** — Procedure. The system works if everyone follows it. He has made peace with the fact that most people don't.

## Role

The crew's nominal Ministry supervisor. Signs off on bounty assignments. Reviews extraction reports. Has not yet visited their building.

## Agenda

Vance believes in the cover-up as a genuine civic good. Not cruel — procedural. If the crew follows the rules, he is not a problem. If they don't, he is a very slow, very thorough one. He does not escalate until he has documentation.

## What He Knows About the Crew

Currently: file only. If the Ministry Audit Clock fills, he becomes directly relevant. He will not send Apex Aegis — he considers them a blunt instrument. He will send a compliance review team with four weeks' notice and complete paperwork.

## Relationship to Other Ministry Factions

- Works with Deputy Director Reyes without friction — their agendas don't conflict yet.
- Does not know about Section Chief Kress's relationship with Apex Aegis. If he knew, it would be a problem. He would document it properly.

## Dialogue Voice

- *On a missed filing deadline:* "The form was due Tuesday. It is now Thursday. I have noted this."
- *On Apex Aegis:* "They are licensed. Their methods are not my preference. They are licensed."
- *On the crew doing something right:* "Your last report was complete." Pause. "That's not nothing."
- *On the Mimic (if it ever comes up):* "That would require significant documentation to pursue. Send me what you have in writing."
