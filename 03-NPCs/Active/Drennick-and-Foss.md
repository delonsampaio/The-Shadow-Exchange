# Drennick & Foss — Apex Aegis Peacekeepers

**The gag:** they are *genuinely dangerous* and *genuinely petty,* in that order. They never stop an argument to start a pursuit — they **finish the thought,** *then* give chase.

| | **Drennick** | **Foss** |
|---|---|---|
| **Look** | Tall, calm, rain-pale; dark coat, earpiece, a clipboard he holds like a menu | Short, intense, pressed jaw; dark coat, earpiece, a second clipboard |
| **Position on the beets** | Defends them. "It's a vegetable. It's not a *threat.*" | "It has a **texture.**" |
| **Quirk** | Never raises his voice; says "Noted." | Reads every sentence like a charge sheet |
| **Competence** | Surveillance; tracking; patience | Interrogation; tactical |
| **Secret** | Wanted to be a chef | **Is right about the beets** |

## The Running Beet Argument *(escalate each appearance)*
1. **S2 (Meridian):** "It's the canteen's *Thursday* beet. It's *braised.*" "It's *wet.*"
2. **S3 (Ashby):** Mid-argument; they arrive finishing a sentence about *the aftertaste.*
3. **S4+:** Foss brings a **thermos of beet soup** to a stakeout out of spite.
4. **Mid-campaign:** They cooperate with the crew *against an Apex Cleaner team* — because the team insulted the beets. (Pay it off. The crew is gobsmacked.)
5. **S12 (The Squeeze, the buyout offer):** Foss, at the worst moment: *"For the record — I was right."* (He is.)

## Voice
- *Opening a pursuit:* "—and *that's* my point. Right. Get them."
- *Reading a charge:* "Unlicensed extraction detected. Meridian block. Second floor commercial." *(in the voice of a form)*
- *Foss, seeing the crew's cover story:* "Municipal Department of — Drennick, is that real?" "...Noted."

## Mechanics
At crew Heat 6+ or standing −2 with Apex, they are joined by **Cleaners** (a more serious team). If the crew has been *funny* to them, Drennick quietly lowers the standing penalty by 1 — a rare mercy.
