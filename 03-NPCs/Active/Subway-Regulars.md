# Subway Regulars — Voice Cards

*Each is a service, a joke, and a hook. Use two per visit.*

## The Conductor
- **Voice:** flat, public-address, a little tired; *never seen.*
- **Role:** announces "delays" that are personal and prophetic. The Omen announcer.
- "The 8:15 to *the call you keep not returning* is delayed."
- "All passengers please note: the platform is **slightly smaller than it was this morning.**"
- *At Omen 8:* "All passengers, please remain calm."

## Dev (Devendra Mensah) — **The Last Stop** (café)
- **Voice:** warm, quick, makes eye contact with the thing next to you.
- **Anchor:** a chipped café mug, 1974.
- "What can I get you? We're out of *the concept of oat milk.*"
- *Clears 1 Stress, or a rumor, once per visit.*
- *🎭 Bit:* makes a flat white for a creature with no mouth — "she just likes to hold it."

## Officer Bram — gargoyle, "Neutral Ground Enforcement"
- **Voice:** gravel and complaint-letter formality.
- "Thirty years without incident. Three hours without moving. I'd appreciate it if you'd keep both numbers where they are."
- *Rules on disputes at length.* Knows who didn't come back from **Past the Yellow Line.**
- *🎭 Bit:* delivers every ruling in the voice of a man filing a formal grievance with himself.

## Madame Thessaly Quill — "Gifts of the Underworld"
- **Voice:** gilded, theatrical, perpetually pitching.
- "A *souvenir!* For the thing you didn't know you needed to remember."
- Stock: **"I ♥ LINE 0" magnets that hum**, a snow globe of the Meridian that is *always snowing,* a keychain that opens exactly one door once.
- *Hook:* one of her trinkets is a shard of the **Ghost Map** (MacGuffin source).

## Wim — Lost & Found
- **Voice:** soft, apologetic, surprisingly funny when he thinks no one is listening.
- "Yes, we have your *lunch.* We also have a *memory.* I'm not sure whose. Would you like to try it?"
- *Hook:* holds a piece of a PC's lost heirloom (or the missing page of the Hunter's Journal) **and the lease to Curios & Chronicles.**

## Ottilie Brand — hunter, Harrow & Sons heir
- **Voice:** competitive, sunny, a little reckless; keeps a **scorecard** of the crew's scores in a pocket notebook.
- "You did the *Barghest?* I'm at four, but two were *vampires,* so they count double."
- *Hook:* a natural Friend *or* Rival for a PC; her grandfather doesn't trust the crew's black-market side; she does.
