# The Fence

**Look** — Never fully lit. Stands behind a partition that has become part of the architecture.
**Mannerism** — Handles items without being asked. Sets them down with a number. Doesn't explain the number.
**Drive** — The market works because someone keeps it honest.

## What They Do

Buys Biologicals and Relics at market rate. Siphoned goods at half price, no questions asked. Asks nothing about provenance. Remembers everything the crew has ever sold.

**Pricing:**
- Standard Biological or Relic: market rate (GM sets per item)
- Siphoned goods: half price — the method is visible to anyone who handles the item
- Ethereal Credits accepted and issued

## Dialogue Voice

- *Appraising an item:* Picks it up. Sets it down. "Four." No unit specified. The unit is always obvious from context.
- *On Siphoned goods:* "Half." A pause. "You know why."
- *On the crew's reputation:* "I've heard things." Doesn't say what. The number changes based on what they were.
- *On a bad item:* "No." Sets it back. Doesn't touch it again.

## Notes

The Fence knows every item that has moved through the Subway for the past thirty years. This is a resource the crew has not yet thought to use as intelligence. When they do, the Fence will trade information — but only information about items, never about people.
