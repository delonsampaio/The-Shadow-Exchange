# Season 1B / 2 Cast Cards (GM ONLY)

**Mortality key** (`Season-1-Plan.md` §4): PROTECTED · HINGE ONLY · CAN DIE · CAN LEAVE. **Voice rule:** every spoken line is a 🗣 SAY the GM reads; the GM never acts anything out. **Menace Rule:** no jokes beside a threat or a death.

## Docks & Daughters (S8–11)

**Emery Vantage** *(dead by S10–11)* — hunter, ex-partner of Dasha Lowe. **Look:** a frayed collar, a thermos and a habit of tapping the table twice. **Wants:** to be right about the pipeline. **Appears:** only as a photograph (Wall of the Missing #1) and in his own journal pages (S8 storage unit). 🗣 **SAY** *(Harlan, about him)*: "He told me it wasn't the monsters. He was always going to be right about that." **Secret:** his last job was routed through Kress's office. **Status:** CAN DIE (dead by S10–11).

**Dasha Lowe** — alive, indexing records for Apex; remembers the originating form number. **Look:** an Apex lanyard turned inside out, ink on her thumb. **Wants:** out, alive, with the proof. 🗣 **SAY** *(quiet, fast)*: "I remember the form number. I'll say it once, and then I was never here." **Secret:** the form number is the second route to *why Emmerich was targeted*, so freeing Artie never costs the clue. **Hook:** take her + Kress's contract chain, or free Artie too. **Status:** CAN DIE / CAN LEAVE.

**Artie Bell** — Twice-Born, a Tier-1 Biological kept siphoned to calibrate Apex scanners. **Anchor:** a child's laminated drawing of a bus, taped inside the scanner cage (his daughter drew it). **Look:** a grey cardigan, hands that shake whenever a machine hums. **Wants:** to see the drawing, once. 🗣 **SAY** *(gentle)*: "She gave me the bus. I never learned which stop." **Hook:** leave him (proof, Twice-Born −1) or free him (+1 Favor, evidence room burns, Kress learns C&C's name). **Status:** CAN DIE / CAN LEAVE.

## The Compass & The Glass Mirrors (Mini-Arc C, S9–14)

**Callum Oates** — Twice-Born, a former sailor. **Anchor:** a brass compass that points to one alley. **Look:** a salt-white beard, a pea coat in all weather. **Wants:** the compass back. 🗣 **SAY** *(slow)*: "It never pointed north. It always pointed to home." **Hook:** return it (+1 Favor) or collapse the mirror bleed (his Anchor unravels). **Status:** CAN LEAVE.

## The Squeeze & The Mask (S12–15)

**Valerie Crane** *(Season 2 opener; unnamed Regional Director until then)* — Apex's Regional Director, replaces the old one. **Look:** a charcoal suit, a smile that arrives a half-second late. **Wants:** the Ghost Map. 🗣 **SAY** *(calm)*: "I never raise my voice. The paperwork does it for me." **Secret:** she holds an Anchor vault of ~30 items (P7 of the Anchor catalog). **Status:** HINGE ONLY.

**Henrik Dane** *(S16)* — Thorne's replacement as the field liaison. **Look:** a stiff collar, a pocket watch he checks aloud. **Wants:** procedure. 🗣 **SAY** *(formal)*: "I am not Mr. Thorne. I will be on time." **Secret:** none; a stiff, honest man (a contrast with Thorne). **Status:** CAN LEAVE.

**Soren Kade** *(S16, the Rival Hunter)* — independent hunter on the Away Job. **Look:** a salvaged leather jacket, scarred knuckles, a grin. **Wants:** to beat the crew to the prize, and half-believes they are wrong about him. 🗣 **SAY** *(easy)*: "You'd be good if you weren't so loud about it." **Hook:** his Architect clue (#9): something the crew "should ask Harlan about." **Status:** CAN DIE / CAN LEAVE.

## Ghosts of Faces Worn (S16–19)

**The real Elias Thorne** — alive on the coast, eleven years displaced. **Look:** a pressed coat, a still face, hands that fold neatly. **Wants:** his badge. 🗣 **SAY** *(rigid)*: "If I get my badge back I will prosecute Kress first and your firm second." **Secret:** he knows something took his life, but not what; nobody believed him. **Status:** HINGE ONLY (the S19 choice).

**Tamsin Solano** *(S17–19)* — the **real** woman whose face the Understudied wore for seven years; a former municipal-infrastructure journalist, now thought to have had a breakdown; she writes a local urban-gardening newsletter and gets a feeling she can't explain near certain buildings. **Look:** gardening gloves, her grandmother's ring (the Understudied kept wearing it; see `The-Casting-Mask.md`). **Wants:** to understand why she lost seven years. 🗣 **SAY** *(warm, puzzled)*: "I covered a survey once. It kept logging a dry pocket. Then I stopped remembering why I cared." **Hook:** her old survey notes (Architect clue #2: the Subway kept appearing over an impossible dry pocket). The S19 choice: give real Thorne and Tamsin new lives, or put Thorne before Vance. **Status:** CAN LEAVE.

## The Meridian Split (S20–23)

**The Harrow Patriarch** — owner of Harrow & Sons; secretly sells hunting records to Apex. **Look:** a signet ring, a cane he doesn't need. **Wants:** to keep the family name. 🗣 **SAY** *(dry)*: "Records are just paper. People are the problem." **Hook:** expose him (the Castellan hunter lives, +1 Favor) or bury it for the Twice-Born address list (used at S27) + 4 Coin. **Status:** HINGE ONLY (S23).

**The Castellans** *(S21)* — one **handler**, one **hunter** (🔒 the hunter is Twice-Born; the handler doesn't know). **Look (hunter):** a battered signet ring never removed; spars at 5 AM. **Wants:** to stay out of the Ministry's sight. 🗣 **SAY** *(hunter — flat)*: "Tell the handler I'm sick. He'll believe that." **Secret:** the ring is his Anchor; seen in plain sight at the Iron-Bell Gym (Venues #24). **Status:** CAN DIE.
