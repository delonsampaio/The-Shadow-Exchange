# The Wall of the Missing — roster

**The wall** (canon, `03-NPCs/Active/Harlan-Okafor.md`): a cork wall of night photographs of hunters in rain slickers and headlamps, candle stubs, ribbons and dried flowers below. **Anyone can add a photo. Anyone can take a case from it.** The crew's names are not on it yet.

**Hard rule:** one sharper photo near the centre (a man in a leather work coat, cane, headlamp cap) carries a note in red ink: **"HE WENT BACK."** It is the same red hand as the Journal's red page. **That photo has no case, no name and no answer in Season 1.** Harlan goes quiet and wipes the counter. (The face appears at S28; identity ~S67; see `09-Threads/Secrets-and-Reveals-Schedule.md` #16.)

**How to use:** when the crew looks at the wall or asks Harlan "what's open?", roll d12 or pick. 🔊 READ ALOUD the photo line, then 🗣 SAY Harlan's one line. A case becomes a job with `10-Generators/Improv-Kit.md` §7 or the linked Bestiary entry. **Never name a monster or its Anchor on screen.** Menace Rule: the photo is sad, not a joke.

## Adding the crew
After a hard job, **Harlan pins a photo of a hunter the crew lost or a case they closed**. The crew's names go on the wall only if a PC dies, retires or is declared missing (it is a ritual, not a threat). When a name goes up: 🔊 *a quiet moment*, Harlan lights the candle, **no jokes**. 🔊 READ ALOUD: "Harlan strikes a match, lights one stub, and sets the photo in the empty space. Nobody talks until the match has burned down."
