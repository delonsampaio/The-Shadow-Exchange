Hunter's KitLoadout reference

The Standard Hunter's Kit

Load (SRD Loadout): before a job pick Light (1–3 items: fast, you blend in), Normal (4–5: you look ready for trouble) or Heavy (6: slow, you look like an operative). During the job you may say you have an item on hand, up to your load. Some items count as two. Italic items do not count toward load.

Item Load What it is for
Rock salt 1 Circles, wards, burning wounds, marking what a thing has touched
Iron nails 1 Barriers; things that hate iron; pinning cloth and doors
Silver rune-thread 1 Binding and mending Anchors; stitching a ward shut
Thick gloves 1 Handling hot, cold or Resonant things
Zip ties 1 Restraints; fast jobs; holding a door closed
Thermal blanket 1 Keeping cold things cold, shock, hiding a shape
Ministry clipboard and badge 1 The cover story, in paper form
The cover story itself 0 (italic: free)
Instant camera 0 (italic: free) Photograph what you hunted for the Trophy Wall
Containment jar (small) 2 A Biological or Anchor you don't want the scanners to find

Not in the kit: lights (a flare, a lamp or a torch is a Flashback or something you buy), weapons beyond what your sheet says, and anything else fancy. Anything not here is a Flashback ("I prepared it earlier") or an Acquire Asset in Downtime (Session 3). Session 1 default: everyone is on Normal load (5). The first time someone needs gear, they pick it: "I have a [item]."
