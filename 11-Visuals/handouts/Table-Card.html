Blades in the Dark · quick reference

1 · Roll the dice

Roll d6s equal to your action rating. 0 dots? Roll 2 and take the lowest. Read the highest die.

6You do it. Clean.
4–5You do it, but there's a cost.
1–3It goes wrong. Something bad happens.
Two 6sCritical: it goes better than expected.

Before every roll the GM names Position and Effect. You may negotiate them.

2 · Position — if it goes wrong

ControlledYou act on your terms. Lesser consequences.
RiskyHead to head, under fire. (Default.) Standard consequences.
DesperateYou overreach. Severe consequences.

Rolling Desperate = mark 1 XP in that action's attribute, win or lose.

End-of-session XP (1 each, 2 if a lot): your playbook trigger (on your sheet) · you expressed your beliefs, drives, heritage or background · you struggled with your vice or a trauma.

3 · Effect — if it goes right

LimitedLess than usual. Clock: 1 tick.
StandardThe normal result. Clock: 2 ticks.
GreatMore than usual. Clock: 3 ticks.

Big problems use a progress clock (4, 6 or 8 segments), not hit points.

4 · Consequences the GM can choose

  • Reduced Effect — it works, but less.
  • Complication — trouble: +Heat, a clock ticks, a new threat.
  • Lost Opportunity — try again a different way.
  • Worse Position — Controlled → Risky → Desperate.
  • Harm — an injury, by level (see 6).

A complication never cancels a roll that succeeded.

5 · Stress — spend it, don't fear it

ResistDecide before you roll. Say "I resist." Then roll your attribute: one die per action in it with at least one dot. Insight = Hunt, Study, Survey, Tinker · Prowess = Finesse, Prowl, Skirmish, Wreck · Resolve = Attune, Command, Consort, Sway. Pay 6 − highest die in Stress. A 6 is free; a critical clears 1. The consequence is reduced or avoided. It always works. Once per consequence.
Push yourself2 Stress for one: +1 die, or +1 Effect, or act while incapacitated.
AssistA teammate spends 1 Stress: you get +1 die. No roll.
Devil's BargainThe GM (or a player) offers +1 die for a complication. You may refuse.
Flashback"I already did that." 0 / 1 / 2 Stress by how plausible and risky the prep was.
Bonus dice capAt most 2: one Assist + either Push or a Devil's Bargain (not both).
ArmorMark an armor box instead of rolling to resist. No Stress. Restored when you pick your next Load.

Stress tops out at 9. The last box means Trauma: you drop out of the scene, come back at 0 Stress, and take a permanent quirk.

6 · Harm

LevelEffect on you
1 LesserReduced Effect when it applies.
2 Moderate−1 die when it applies.
3 SevereCan't act without help or a Push.
4 FatalOut. A full row pushes harm up a level.

7 · A score, start to finish

  1. Plan: pick a plan + one detail. Pick your Load.
  2. Engagement roll sets where you start.
  3. Play the score. Flashback instead of planning.
  4. Downtime: clear Stress (Vice), heal, reduce Heat, train.

No initiative. The GM never rolls. Every roll is yours.
