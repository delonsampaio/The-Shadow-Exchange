# Old City Mercy Hospital — Session 6, *The John Doe*

**One-line read** — A tired municipal hospital with a long-stay ward where a Twice-Born patient has been in Room 412 for years with no memory and no name.

## Sensory Profile
* **Lighting:** Fluorescent tubes that hum a half-tone flat; a night corridor lit like an aquarium.
* **Smell:** Floor wax, boiled sugar-tea, and (in 412 only) river water.
* **Ambient Sound:** A ventilator two rooms away; a trolley with one bad wheel; a vending machine that drops everything on the second try.
* **Detection:** Hospitals have witnesses everywhere; **the Overhang's +1 spill rule does not apply**, but a witness roll is needed for any obvious Resonance.

## Key NPCs & Features
* **Reverend Josiah Kemble** *(bench NPC #3)*: night chaplain; cardigan over his collar, a thermos of mostly sugar tea. The human door into the scene. 🗣 **SAY** *(soft, unhurried)*: "Room 412 asks me the same question every night. I don't have an answer that's good enough." He will tell the crew things the hospital won't **if they come back a second night.**
* **The John Doe (Room 412):** Twice-Born, no name, no memory, in a long-stay bed. **Anchor:** a folded paper boat he keeps under his pillow *(the GM may substitute another small object)*. He hums; when he is calm the room smells of river water. **What he knows:** once his memory is restored he **saw the Architect** (Architect knowledge source). 🔒 The Architect's identity stays undecided (candidates A–D in `The-Architect.md`): he gives only fragments true for every candidate (see below).
* **The Ministry (Oversight):** Deputy Director Reyes handed the crew this lead *before* Kress's extraction team (S6 scene); the crew has a head start.
* **The Ministry (Suppression):** Kress's extraction team (a van and two orderlies) is on a clock. 🔒 The crew never meets Kress.
* **A Memory Remediation nurse** *(generic, unnamed; **not** Hester Lyle, who is Reyes's standby and stays on the bench while Reyes is active)*: the quiet hand that "tidies" what patients remember. She can be an ally or a hazard.
* **Greta Fenn** can supply **Form 141-J (Unidentified Patient, Resonance-Adjacent)**: *"Room number, and the square footage of the room."*

## The scene (default beats, S6)
1. 🔊 **READ ALOUD:** "The fourth floor is quiet in the way a library is quiet at closing. A trolley stops. A night nurse, then no one. Through the glass of Room 412 a man in a hospital gown sits upright, hands folded around nothing."
2. 🗣 **SAY** *(Kemble)*: "He hums. It's the same four notes. I keep waiting for the fifth."
3. ❓ **ASK:** "How do you get into Room 412?" ⏸ REACTION STOP. *(Then, after they are in:)* ❓ **ASK:** "What do you tell him?"
4. **Restoring his memory:** needs the Anchor (the paper boat), a quiet hour, or the right word. A player rolls **Consort or Attune** (Risky / Standard, or Controlled with the boat): **4–5** a fragment, then panic; **6** he tells the crew what he saw.
5. 🔒 **The fragments (true for every Architect candidate):** "A red pen." "Red ink that was still wet years later." "A feeling of being *chosen*, like a name read off a list." Do **not** name a person.
6. **Kress's clock:** a 4-segment **Extraction** clock. Each loud move, each Detection tick or each hour in the building fills a segment; at 4 the van arrives at the front door and the nurses start moving beds. Reyes' folder bought about two hours.
7. **Choices:** hand him to the Ministry (Oversight route: he is registered and kept; Reyes owes a favour); **smuggle him to the Subway** (Twice-Born Favor +1; he is safe below and the Ministry loses his trail); leave him (Kress takes him; the clue is lost for months).

## Lethal path (`05-Sessions/Lethal-Path-Quick-Table.md`)
Killing a patient or a staffer: +2 Heat, Detection +2 (witnesses), Twice-Born −1; Kemble will not help again.

## AI Generation Directives
* **Read-Aloud Generation:** quiet, tired, humane. **No jokes** at the bedside (Menace Rule); comedy can come from the vending machine or the orderly arguing about parking.
* **Location Hazard:** a Mixed Success here often costs a *witness*, a nurse who saw too much.
* **Never** name the Architect or say "Omen" on screen.
