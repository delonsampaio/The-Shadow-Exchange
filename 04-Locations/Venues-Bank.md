**How to use:** pick a venue by district and by what the crew needs (Blades downtime activity in the "offers" column). Owners marked *(canon)* or *(NPC-Bench #N)* already exist; "new" owners are `INVENTED` here and go in `03-NPCs/NPC-Index.md` the moment they are met (Change-Propagation-Map). - **Aldric's voice** (Sal's Chair & Shave radio #9, Pier 3 Radio #47, Croft's Shortwave Attic): static and breadcrumbs from S4; the voice itself only after the S9 contact; Croft's line *"It found the frequency"* is **S24+ only** (it is the Dead Air arc, S25).
- **Corvin Pryce's locker (#20 Sterling Safe):** the rumour is fine early, but the file unlocks no earlier than **S16–17** (the Pryce Files arc).
- **LINE 0 on a prop** (Quarter-Drop Arcade #30 high-score screen; Miller's Salvage bus #1147): **Omen 3+ only**, and the bus roll is the *reveal of Episode 1 (The 11:47 Bus)*: use the graveyard as that episode's location or after it, never before.
- **Heron Point Lake House** (Gazetteer, out of town): gate to **S16+** (it holds a vanished archivist's pre-1970 surveys; overlaps the Pryce/Senna archive thread).
- **Meridian Deeds Annex / Sable & Morrell (#15, #7):** the lease stays unreadable until S24 (`FILE PULLED — ARCHIVE HOLD`; a dissolved 1924 bank).
- **Thorne:** no venue may show the signature mismatch before Tell 8 (S12–13) or name the Understudied.
**Standby NPCs here are local cover only:** Mercy (Spin Cycle 24), Yardley (Pike's), Winnie (Community Hall), Hester (Nurse Hester's Parlor), Kip (Glass Conservatory) do **not** grant the original NPC's role while that NPC lives.

## 7. Warnings & Guardrails

### A. Canon Clash Checks (Enforced Here — Watch in Play)
1. **Zero Second Basements & Water Table Rule:** Per Canon-Change-Log-2026-11-20.md, nothing inside the city is dug deeper than a single pumped cellar (~11 steps). All 60 venues, 21 purveyors, and 15 out-of-town sites obey this (even Sterling Safe & Deposit is ground-floor, St. Clare's Abbey uses above-ground vaults, and Siltwater Quarry floods to 10 ft below rim). **Warning:** never improvise an "old smuggling tunnel" or "sub-basement vault" under any venue; if a deep dry room ever appears (like S22 *Basement Two*), it must register as an alarming, impossible anomaly.
2. **Elevated Train Stays Off the City Map:** Per Living-Map.md rule 8, **The Ring El** runs along the outer ring road and must **never** be drawn on `map-city-sNN.png`. Keep its 8 stations strictly on the optional standalone transit diagram (`map-transit-elevated.png`).
3. **Subway Access & Addresslessness:** Never tie any surface venue (such as Spin Cycle 24 or Quarter-Drop Arcade) to a *fixed* Subway entrance. An entrance can appear beside a venue once, never permanently, and **never** before the Emmerich qualifying moment (S2). Before S2, surface fencing runs strictly through low-rate middlemen like **Yardley Pike** (Venue #38) or unnamed nervous drops.
4. **No Train Boarding Before Arc IV:** Even on the Ring El vs. Line 0 contrast, remind players gently if they try to "follow the Ring El onto Line 0" — the two networks do not intersect, and Line 0 has no boardable train until ~S78–80 (only the single S28 pass-by).
5. **NPC-Bench Standby Guardrail:** Six bench NPCs (`Mercy Adeyemi`, `Yardley Pike`, `Winnie Achterberg`, `Hester Lyle`, `Kip Hollander`, `Marigold Oyelaran`) are **standbys** in NPC-Bench.md. In the tables above, the five surface standbys appear strictly in minor local roles and must **not** grant the canon NPC's signature mechanical privilege (e.g., Mercy does not grant Harlan's 24–48h bounty lead; Yardley pays only 1/3 vs. The Fence's market rate) unless the original canon NPC dies or leaves per Season-1-Plan.md §4.
6. **Harlan's Diner Isolation:** None of the 60 city venues know Harlan's exact highway address except Mercy Adeyemi (who refuses to speak of him) and old-guard hunters who never write it down — keeping Apex from locating the diner before ~S41.
7. **Terminology Discipline:** Keep "Resonance" (never "magic," "spell," or "mana") across all venue read-alouds, including *Madame Vera's Palmistry* and *Weird* vice purveyors.
