# The Living Subway

*Companion to `Overview.md` (the entrance), `Stations.md` (the first descent) and `Mysteries.md` (the four truths). This file is what makes it a **place**.*

**Design goal:** the crew should *want* to come down — for coffee, gossip, work, comfort — so that when the Decay Clock bites, they feel it as loss, not just a number.

---

## 1. The Five Courtesies (the rules everyone *knows* and no one wrote down)

1. **No blood.** Violence in the Subway is not forbidden — it is *impossible to survive the aftermath of*. The lights go out; the stranger who threw the punch is outside, on a pier, with no memory of the stair.
2. **No taking.** Nothing leaves an owner's hand without a price. (This includes Anchors. *Especially* Anchors.)
3. **No names without leave.** You don't say a Twice-Born's name unless they've given it. Titles only until offered.
4. **Settle up.** Debts to PTOLEMY are paid before you leave, or you're *tracked.*
5. **Don't ask it who runs this place.** Everyone is polite about that.

> **Breaking a Courtesy:** the Subway doesn't punish — it *notices.* Standing drops, doors "forget" to open, PTOLEMY's price goes up. Repeated: access can fade (see Decay).

---

## 2. Economy

- **Ethereal Credits (EC):** scrip printed on old transit transfers. Accepted only below. **1 EC ≈ 1 Coin inside the Subway.** Earned from non-government jobs and the Fence.
- **Favors:** the real currency. PTOLEMY's ledger, the Twice-Born Favor tokens, and social debts are all the same ledger.
- **Barter:** most stalls prefer an item, an errand, or a *story the stallholder hasn't heard.*
- **Money from above:** surface Coin works *at a markup* (about 1 Coin → ⅔ EC) — the Subway doesn't love the surface.

## 5. What the Crew Can *Do* Down There

**New Downtime activity — "Subway Visit"** (counts as one free activity; **Consort**):
- **6:** pick one — a service at half cost, a rumor from the d8 table, or +1 Favor with a chosen NPC.
- **4–5:** one service at normal cost *or* a rumor — **plus** a complication (d8 Encounter).
- **1–3:** you owe PTOLEMY a charge, and the d8 Encounter happens at its worst.

| Service | Who | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| **Sell Biologicals/Relics** | The Fence | Market rate; Siphoned = half; EC accepted |
| **Craft permanent gear** | The Workshop | **6-segment project clock**, Anchor-safe; +1 Tier quality |
| **Identify mystery items / Anchors** | PTOLEMY | The Appraiser's Gamble (Study/Consort) |
| **Intel trade** | The Whisperer | Info for info; reveals which bounties are **manufactured** |
| **Find a lost thing** | Wim (Lost & Found) | Study to describe it; may return something unasked |
| **Calm down** | Dev's café | Clear 1 Stress / free rumor |
| **Take a non-gov job** | The Job Board | See table below |
| **Make a deal** | The Broker | Cost d6 (secret); *Session 8+ unless provoked* |
| **Safe harbor / lore / anchor intervention** | Twice-Born (Favors) | See `The-Twice-Born.md` |

### Job Board — d6 *(non-government jobs; no Ministry/Apex oversight)*
1. *A brass compass that always points to Calloway Alley.* (Pays: the story of how it got there. → The sailor's Anchor.)
2. *"Someone has been asking about Line 0 above ground. No uniform. Twice in two weeks."* (Warning; the Mimic mapping entrances.)
3. *Escort a very nervous Twice-Born to a wedding.* 🎭 (Complication: the wedding is a monster wedding.)
4. *Retrieve something from the Lost & Found that Wim says "isn't lost."*
5. *Sealed envelope, red pin.* (PTOLEMY's clasps click once. **Not tonight.** → Architect.)
6. *A pet-sitting job.* The pet has opinions.

### Rumor table — d8
1. Apex is paying hunters to find the entrances. Those hunters can't find them.
2. A merchant on Platform 1 *hasn't been there since Tuesday.* No one knows who she was.
3. The Conductor said something strange about *Thursday* and *soup.* (→ Session 3.)
4. Someone has been seen at the Turnstiles with **two faces** — not at once.
5. PTOLEMY undercharged someone. Nobody knows why. It's unsettling.
6. The Main Hall is "a little smaller" than it was last month.
7. A hunter came back from Past the Yellow Line. They won't speak. They have *started drawing.* (Omen 7 foreshadow.)
8. The map's red ink moved overnight.

### Encounter table — d8 *(🎭 = comedy, 🔴 = dread)*
1. 🎭 **A monster in a suit** asks the crew to settle a dispute: who really owns the good armrest.
2. 🎭 **Officer Bram** rules, at length, that someone "loitered with intent."
3. 🎭 **The Conductor** announces a delay *for one of the PCs by name.*
4. 🔴 **A Twice-Born recognizes the Anchor the crew carries** and goes very quiet.
5. 🎭 **Madame Quill** sells a PC something that is already theirs.
6. 🔴 **The map's red ink** spreads while the crew watches.
7. 🎭 **Ottilie Brand** challenges the crew to a "scorecard" bet.
8. 🔴 **A stranger in Thorne's coat** passes at the far end of the platform. (It's a bystander. *Almost always.*)

### The Turnstiles — where you come out (d6)
1. A pier over water, brick wall, cigarette ad (1973). 2. A laundromat at 3 am. 3. A cemetery gate. 4. The back of a closed theater. 5. A bus shelter, mid-route (the bus does not stop). 6. **Your own front door** *(the Subway noticed you were tired)*.

## 6. Decay Stages — the Subway *shrinking and dimming* (the "Decay Clock" made visible)

| Decay | What the crew *sees and hears* |
|---|---|
| **0–1** | Normal. A few vendors a little nervous. The red line on the map is one finger long. |
| **2** | The Conductor's announcements get quieter. Dev's café closes an hour early. |
| **3** | A Turnstile stops working. Someone has stopped being on Platform 2. |
| **4** *(mile-marker)* | **Merchants pack up. Twice-Born flee.** The Main Hall is *visibly smaller*; the crowd thins; Wim is shouting for "someone to claim this." Apex's intelligence begins triangulating. (Lose a Favor if holding 3.) |
| **5** | Entire platforms dark. The red line reaches the Main Hall. Officer Bram has moved — for the first time in thirty years. |
| **6** | **The cover-up collapses.** The Subway's last act: it opens every Turnstile at once, across the city, in the daytime. |

*(Which truth is real — Mysteries A–D — changes *why*: A the Subway is dying; B the builder is returning; C the controlling intelligence is being unmasked; D the community is leaving.)*

## 8. Co-GM Directives for the Subway
- Two or three sensory details per visit, not a tour.
- Every visit must include **one funny beat and one thing that *costs* something** (a price, a rumor, a Favor).
- When Decay moves, change the *place* first (a stall gone, a platform dark), then tell them the number.
- Never let the Subway answer *who runs it* with words.
