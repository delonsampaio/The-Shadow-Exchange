# Curios & Chronicles

**One-line read** — A narrow, wood-paneled bookstore that feels like a quiet fort, built inside a dead savings bank, hiding a monster-processing suite. Twenty dollars a day, on purpose.

---

## Canon Layout (memorize this — it resolves every earlier contradiction)

```
 ROOF            — access hatch; Apex drones can't see under the overhang
 3rd FLOOR       — the Lofts: three bunks, a clothes line, a window onto an airshaft (sleeping, and where PCs keep personal stuff)
 2nd FLOOR       — the Annex: kitchenette & hot plate, mismatched armchairs, the Whiteboard of Things We Don't Say
 GROUND FLOOR    — THE FRONT SHOP (public) → Zoltar (the door) 
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 BEHIND ZOLTAR   — a short iron stair (eleven steps) down into THE CONTAINMENT SUITE
                   ONE long, low, vaulted room. Everything below is in it.
```

> **History (reveal gradually):** the building was a **savings bank branch**, 1920s. The "vault" is a real bank vault. The shop makes **$20 a day**. Someone on the crew will point out the irony within five minutes of hearing it. Let them.

**Crew history (canon):** the crew have worked here **two years**. They *chose* the building; *no one remembers who signed the lease* (the rent is paid through a mechanism none of them fully understand — an Architect hook; see `Plot-Threads.md`).

## First Entry Read-Aloud — The Shop *(Session 0)*

> "The shop is narrow enough that the shelves on either side would brush your shoulders if you reached out both arms. Floor-to-ceiling books, all of them real, all of them organized by a system that appears to be personal. The smell is vanilla and old parchment and floor wax and something underneath those, faintly metallic, that you have learned to recognize.
>
> A rotary phone on the counter. A half-knitted sweater on the reading chair that no one is in. A cat on the counter — large, grey, profoundly indifferent. The tag on her collar reads **CONSEQUENCE.**
>
> There is a door behind the main shelving unit. It is not hidden, exactly. But first-time visitors consistently describe the shop as having no back room."

## The Front Shop (public)

**Purpose:** cover. Thirty feet deep, fifteen wide. Every book is genuine stock. The till is manual. The phone works — **Ministry couriers use it first.**

- **The Rotary Phone:** the only publicly listed number. Sometimes it rings twice at 3 am with no one there.
- **Consequence:** the cat. Has *never*, not once, reacted to any supernatural event in the shop. The crew finds this reassuring. *(Running gag: she is always asleep on the most inconvenient object — see `Comedy-Playbook.md`.)*
- **The Front Window:** permanently dusty, on purpose.
- **The Occult & Folklore Section:** stocked, ironically, with *accurate* books. Customers buy them as novelty gifts.
- **The Zoltar Machine:** an old fortune-teller cabinet, back corner, behind moldering atlases. Glass front, robed automaton, painted beard, turban, brass hands on a velvet pedestal. Unplugged for decades. **Coin slot looks dead.** Push the crew's coin sequence and: glass eyes flicker once, green; head turns one degree left; the wall behind him slides back with the pneumatic hiss of a train door.
  - **🎭 The Coin Code:** the table invents the sequence in Session 0. It is canon forever. Zoltar visibly *judges* a wrong attempt (a small sad whirr).
  - **🎭 Fortune cards:** at the end of every session, a card drops from the slot anyway. See `Comedy-Playbook.md`.

**Cover protocol:** if a mundane customer enters during an active situation, the crew has ~90 seconds. Someone sits in the reading chair and picks up the sweater; someone helps the customer; nobody says "basement."

## The Upper Floors (new — gives the players a *home*)

### The Annex (2nd floor)
Kitchenette, hot plate (tea is always on), three mismatched armchairs, a corkboard of takeout menus. The **Whiteboard of Things We Don't Say** lists phrases banned in the shop ("basement," "exsanguinate," "that's not a dog"). Whenever a player breaks it, the table adds a tally mark. *(Free comedy generator.)*

### The Lofts (3rd floor)
Three bunks (one per PC), a window onto an airshaft, clothes line, a locked footlocker each. **Each PC's corner is where their Heirloom lives** — ask in Session 0: *"What's on your shelf?"*

## The Containment Suite (behind Zoltar) — one long, low, cold room

> **Entering:** an iron stair, eleven steps down. The door seals behind you and the bookshop is gone. The smell changes: vanilla and old parchment replaced by a chemical tang — the clean you only smell in dental offices and morgues. Fluorescent panels click on overhead, one by one.

| Fixture | Description | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold Storage Locker** | A bank of stainless steel drawers along the far wall, each inscribed with thin silver runes. Pull one open and the cold fogs. Two of the four compartments require gloves. Things that should spoil don't — for months. | Pauses **Biological Spoilage** |
| **Lead-Lined Vault** | The original bank vault door set into the brick: heavy as a small car, lead-lined, a row of small mirrors sunk flush into the face. Fifteen seconds to open fully. Sealed, whatever's inside goes quiet — stops humming, stops calling, stops appearing on any scanner. *The mirrors are not for looking at.* Combination known to the full crew. | Contents emit **no Resonance**; locked if **Audit** fills |
| **The Post-Op Table** | Zinc-topped, leather straps bolted at the corners; the zinc is scratched in patterns that suggest something was very interested in getting up. The crew does not discuss where it came from. | Tinker (field surgery); first-stage harvesting |
| **The Scanner Station** | Half-buried in cabling: **three modified shortwave radios** on a rolling cart, each tuned to a government frequency (they intercept Ministry bounty traffic before the public list updates) — plus a **fourth, older unit with one green lamp** that has not been off in two years: the Resonance detector, reach ≈ two blocks. A soft wash of static at all hours. On clear nights: *a voice on AM that sounds like another state and can't be triangulated — never wrong about where.* | Tinker for a specific signature; Survey for general detection |
| **The Trophy Wall** | A corkboard on the back wall pinned with **polaroids**, each annotated in red marker — a date, a name, a cause of death that is *not* the one the Ministry filed. Every hunt this building has ever witnessed. The earliest photo is older than one of the crew. None of them pinned it. | **+1d Study** for a monster type already hunted. After the **first hunt of a new type**, someone photographs it (the instant camera, free) and pins the polaroid; from then on the +1d applies to that type. Auto-Heat if the Ministry raids |
| **The Hunter's Journal** | Old leather-bound journal on a reading stand near the Post-Op table. Half the handwriting is the crew's; half isn't. Two generations of entries pre-date them. The handwriting changes at entry 47 with no explanation. **One entry, in red ink, four years old, in a hand no one recognizes (🔒 on the TV (`item-hunters-journal.png`) the red line is scribbled over and unreadable; its single line, readable only when the crew studies it: **"ASK THE ARCHITECT BEFORE YOU SIGN."**)** (→ Architect thread). | **+1d Study** when consulted before a score vs. a known type. Lost if confiscated |
| **The Wall of Rules** *(new)* | Hand-lettered on the plaster by the stair, in five different hands: **1.** Nobody says "basement." **2.** Cold things go in the cold place. *Today.* **3.** If it hums, it goes in the vault. **4.** If it sings, it goes in the vault *and you leave.* **5.** Do not feed Consequence from the evidence. **6.** *(Added in a sixth hand, in red:)* Do not trust anything that offers you tea. *(→ Session 3 payoff.)* | Onboarding + comedy: when a rule is broken, someone adds a tally |
| **The Late-Fees Drawer** *(new)* | A drawer of overdue library cards from every customer who has borrowed a book and not returned it. Nobody collects. It is the only "ledger" the shop keeps that isn't an Anchor ledger. | Cover; gag |

**What new crew members are told on day one:** *"The Cold Storage pauses the spoilage clock — anything biological goes in there immediately, not tomorrow. The vault is lead-lined; nothing inside pings a scanner. Do not leave Relics in the Post-Op or the front shop. Resonance bleeds. The neighbors don't report gas leaks, but they do talk."*
