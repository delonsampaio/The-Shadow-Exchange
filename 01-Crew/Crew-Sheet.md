# Curios & Chronicles — Crew Sheet

A struggling bookstore on a narrow brick lot between two glass-and-steel skyscrapers, in a former savings bank. Vanilla, old parchment, floor wax. A rotary phone, a half-knitted sweater, a sleeping cat named Consequence. Twenty dollars a day on purpose. *The crew has worked here two years; nobody remembers who signed the lease.*

### What kept items do *(Double Bookkeeping loot; GM 2026-11-22)*
A kept item is not just a trophy. Between scores the crew may use it in one of these ways:

| Kept item | Sell | Use on a score | Bribe or leverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Biological** | Alchemists or the Fence, about 3–4 Coin; only while fresh (Cold Storage keeps it fresh) | **One use, then consumed:** its own effect (Barghest Gland +1d **and +1 effect level** on one Finesse, Prowl, Skirmish or Wreck roll · Ethereal Gland: one action can harm something incorporeal · Vampire Blood reduces one harm by one level (a level 1 harm is cleared) · Gorgon Scalp petrifies one threat for one action (no roll to trigger; it cannot act or inflict harm in that beat) · Phase-Shifter Heart: one wall). No Load | Gift it for a Favor, or put its value toward a bribe |
| **Relic** | The Fence pays about 4 Coin (half if Siphoned) | Its own power, once per score; then Resonance +1 (leaks haunt the lair, see Inventory) | Counts as about 4 Coin toward a bribe (e.g. paying down the Audit); or buys a faction's goodwill, with the risk that they want more |
| **Anchor** | Never sold | Never used as a tool | Leverage only: sparing it earns a Twice-Born Favor, but a kept Anchor leaves its owner without cover (Anchor Cover Rule). Carrying one near Apex gets you flagged |

**Bring one:** before a score the crew may name **one** kept Biological or Relic to bring along. It costs no Load, and it is not then sold or used as a bribe; if it is not used on the score it comes home (a Biological taken out of Cold Storage starts its Spoilage clock: teach Ladder card 19 then, even before S4; it ticks once per Downtime and twice per scene on an Away Job, and Cold Storage pauses it). Only one kept item per bounty (Double Bookkeeping). The Casting Mask is never a kept item.
