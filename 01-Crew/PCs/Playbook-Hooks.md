# Playbook Hooks — Make Every PC Matter

Three players, seven playbooks. Use only the rows for the playbooks actually chosen. **Rule of thumb:** every session gives each PC **one spotlight moment** and **one comedy niche.** Tick them on the dashboard Gags panel.

## Per-Playbook Notes

### Cutter
- **Spotlight beats:** *S1* — the Surge: the person who *holds Merton* without hurting him. *S2* — the Door Chase: the one who braces a door against Apex. *S3* — Mabel's furniture: moving a heavy sideboard that "has been there since 1952."
- **Ask the player:** "What's the one thing your character will not hit, ever?" *(Answer becomes a trauma hook.)*
- **Friend/Rival ideas (campaign-safe):** Harlan *(Friend)*; a gym owner whose class they keep skipping; Ottilie Brand *(Rival)*.

### Hound
- **Spotlight:** *S1* — **Gerald**: the Hound is the first to notice the dog's hands are *warm* (Resonance). *S2* — tracking Thorne's route in the crowd (Tell 3 spotted *by them*). *S3* — the pier (finding where the exit goes).
- **Ask the player:** "What animal does your character trust more than people?"
- **Friend/Rival:** a vet who owes them; Foss *(Rival, 🎭)*.

### Leech
- **Spotlight:** *S1* — the Gland: clean extraction *or* the heart-wrenching refusal. *S2* — the thimble: they can tell it's *warm* (Anchor). *S3* — the needle; the Grief Jars (alchemical value) — and the choice *not* to sell.
- **Ask the player:** "What's your character's most illegal invention — and what is it *actually* for?"
- **Friend/Rival:** a pharmacist; a guild chemist; Wim *(Friend, later)*.

### Lurk
- **Spotlight:** *S1* — the locker (they find the photo first). *S2* — the Door Chase: the boutique. *S3* — the house: moving through Mabel's walls *with* Mabel's commentary.
- **Ask the player:** "What's the worst place your character has ever hidden — and for how long?"
- **Friend/Rival:** a locksmith; Drennick *(Rival — he's the one who always finds them)*.

### Slide
- **Spotlight:** *S1* — **Cover Story Roulette** (the badge). *S2* — Thorne on the street (they are the one who *clocks the half-second*). *S3* — Agnes: social triage while Mabel decides.
- **Ask the player:** "What's the lie your character tells most often — and who believes it?"
- **Friend/Rival:** a journalist; Stalls *(Rival, S4)*; Greta *(Friend, 🎭: "we've exchanged forms for two years")*.

### Spider
- **Spotlight:** *S1* — the plan *and* the Flashback. *S2* — Emmerich's choice: the Spider who **sees two moves ahead and still can't decide.** *S3* — the Acquire Assets session: they love it.
- **Ask the player:** "What's your character's biggest plan that *almost* worked?"
- **Friend/Rival:** an old mentor; Kress *(Rival — but they don't know it yet; plant via paperwork)*.

### Whisper
- **Spotlight:** *S1* — Merton's collar: only they can *feel* what the leak is. *S2* — the Subway: Resonance *silence* is the scariest thing they've ever felt. *S3* — **Mabel**: they're the natural "I know" voice; the Telling is their scene.
- **Ask the player:** "What did your character hear the first time they realized the dead are polite?"
- **Friend/Rival:** a hospice nurse; **Cosma Linden** *(after Session 2)*.

## Banter Pairings *(have the table pick at Session 0 — then reuse)*
Each pair gets **one running bit.** Examples: *the one who always overpacks vs. the one who never packs; the planner vs. the improviser; the optimist vs. the one who read the Journal.* Put them in the dashboard **Gags** panel.

## Heirloom Spotlight *(one per PC per arc)*
Each Heirloom stage change is a **mini-scene**, not a mechanic. Use `Heirloom-Degradation-Patterns.md`. Make sure each PC gets **one heirloom moment by Session 6.**

## Spotlight Tracker *(Co-GM: check every session)*
- Did each PC roll in the key scene? Did each get a **choice**? Did each get a **laugh**?
- If one is quiet: hand them the next 🎭 beat.
