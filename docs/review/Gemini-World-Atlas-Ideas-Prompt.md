# Gemini World-Atlas and Venues Ideas Prompt

Paste the block into Gemini (it has local file access and web search). Claude triages the result per `Gemini-Triage-Protocol.md`: **Gemini suggests, Claude decides**; every real-world claim needs a source link and is spot-checked, and anything that contradicts the canon in `docs/review/Canon-Change-Log-2026-11-20.md` is rejected.

**Why:** the crew needs places to *go*: for information, downtime, supplies, hiding, healing and indulging their vices. `04-Locations/` has the main sites; this pass widens the map into a gazetteer players can wander through, and lists the maps that will be needed. An Opus reviewer is separately cataloguing existing locations (`docs/review/draft-content/07-Locations-and-Sites-Catalog.md`); compare, do not duplicate.

````
ROLE
You are a worldbuilder and Blades in the Dark GM-coach. You write gazetteers for noir monster-hunting campaigns: evocative, specific, and immediately usable at the table.

THE CAMPAIGN
A GM plus three friends with 20+ years of tabletop experience play Blades in the Dark weekly (80 sessions in three seasons). Tone: Supernatural / Scooby-Doo comedy with real dread (monsters are scary; the comedy lives in the people). Setting: a modern, rain-slick city ("the city"), five districts: Old City (walled historic core, where the crew's bookshop front is), The Meridian (glass towers and shopping, the most Apex scanners), The Fairground District (theaters and late nights), The Docks (industrial, salt and rust, a harbor and piers), The Overhang (terraced hillside apartments where neighbors report everything). Canon facts: the city has NO subway (the water table is about a storey down); travel is by car, bike, taxi, bus and one elevated train; the "Subway" is a magical hidden place that appears for the chosen and has no fixed address; nothing is dug deeper than a single pumped cellar; Resonance (not "magic") is the in-world word. Crew type: Hunters, with a Ministry front and a black market. It is a private table of friends, so real brand names are fine.

READ (repo-relative paths)
04-Locations/City-Districts.md, Curios-and-Chronicles.md, The-Subway/ (all), Sites-of-Interest/; 05-Sessions/Season-1-Plan.md; 06-Scores/Score-Catalog.md; 11-Visuals/Living-Map.md (placement plan and map roadmap); 02-Factions/ and 03-NPCs/ (so venues can be owned by existing NPCs where it fits); docs/review/Canon-Change-Log-2026-11-20.md; 00-Canon/Blades-in-the-Dark-SRD.md (downtime activities and vices).

DO THIS
1. VENUE BANK: for EACH of the five districts, propose 12 venues the crew may visit (60 total). Mix: information sources (a library, an archive, a newspaper morgue, a barber, a diner), downtime venues, supply and Acquire-Asset places (a pawnbroker, a surplus shop, a locksmith), healers and Recover options, safehouses and hideouts, Reduce-Heat contacts, hangouts and bars. For each: name; one-line look; owner or regular (an existing canon NPC where it fits, else a new name); what it OFFERS in Blades terms (which downtime activity it serves, what information it can sell, what asset or favour); one secret or hook that can plant a thread; which season it suits.
2. VICE PURVEYORS: for EACH of the seven Blades vices (Faith, Gambling, Luxury, Obligation, Pleasure, Stupor, Weird), propose 3 purveyors in different districts, with a name, a look, and a one-line complication (Heat, a rival, a secret).
3. OUT OF TOWN: 15 places beyond the city limits (the outskirts and ring road, the coast, a small Away-Job town, a roadside motel, an airfield, a rest stop, a quarry, a lake house, and so on), each with a one-line hook and the kind of score it suits.
4. GAZETTEER: 25 street names, bridges and landmarks across the districts, each with a one-line in-world history or legend; the elevated train line with its station names; two or three local newspapers or radio stations; 10 pieces of local slang and 10 ordinary-city details (weather, seasons, transit habits) that make the city feel lived in.
5. MAPS NEEDED: list every map the campaign will want (district zoom maps, site floor plans, an elevated-train map, out-of-town maps, Subway hall guide), say what each should show, and rank them by when they are needed (see the map roadmap in 11-Visuals/Living-Map.md). For the five district zoom maps, list the 12 venues each should label.
6. DOWNTIME MENU: for a crew of three, suggest 20 downtime scenes tied to specific venues (one line each) that also surface information or a thread.
7. WARNINGS: flag anything that clashes with canon, anything too close to a real place or real tragedy, and any venue that would let the crew bypass a planned mystery.

DELIVERABLES: tables per district (venues), the vice-purveyor table, the out-of-town list, the gazetteer, the maps-needed list, the downtime menu, and the warnings. Keep each venue to about 5 lines.

RULES
- Do NOT edit any files. Report only.
- Do not duplicate places already in 04-Locations/ or 06-Scores/Score-Catalog.md; mark every invented place as INVENTED and every real-world reference with a source URL.
- No safety-tool pages; the GM never acts anything out; keep each venue usable by a GM reading live with an AI co-GM.
````
