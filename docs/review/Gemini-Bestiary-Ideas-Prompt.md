# Gemini Bestiary and Urban-Legend Ideas Prompt

Paste the block into Gemini (it has local file access and web search). Claude triages the result per `Gemini-Triage-Protocol.md`: **Gemini suggests, Claude decides**; every folklore claim must carry a source link and is spot-checked, and anything that breaks the Menace Rule, the Three-Layer rule or a GM decision is rejected.

**Why:** an 80-session campaign needs far more one-session monsters than the ten Urban Legends and ten episodes in the repo. Gemini's strength here is grounded research into real folklore, urban legends and creepypasta-style modern legends, which gives the table variety it won't guess from memory.

````
ROLE
You are a folklorist and tabletop-RPG monster designer who writes industrial-occult noir with comic relief. You can search the web: use it to ground creatures in real folklore and real urban legends, and cite the source for each (a URL).

THE TABLE AND TONE
A GM plus three friends with 20+ years of tabletop experience play Blades in the Dark weekly, in a monster-hunting campaign set in a modern city (called "the city"; five districts: Old City, The Meridian, The Fairground District, The Docks, The Overhang). Tone: Supernatural / Scooby-Doo humor with real dread. The monsters must be SCARY; the comedy lives in the people and the bureaucracy, never in the threat itself. Every monster follows the THREE-LAYER RULE: Appears as (a mundane cover) / Is (what it actually is, with an Anchor Item and a Holy Weakness) / Became this because (a human tragedy or reason). The city has no subway (high water table). Resonance (not "magic") is the in-world word. A private table of friends: real brand names are fine.

READ (repo-relative paths)
10-Generators/Urban-Legends.md, Three-Layer-Monster.md, Resonance-Complications.md; 06-Scores/Score-Catalog.md (everything already planned: do NOT duplicate any creature or premise there); 05-Sessions/Season-1-Plan.md; 00-Canon/Comedy-Playbook.md (the Menace Rule at the end), Tone-and-Style.md; 04-Locations/City-Districts.md.

DO THIS
1. CREATURES: propose 40 NEW one-session monsters or urban legends. For each give: name; ORIGIN (real folklore with a source URL and the culture it comes from, or "invented"); the Three Layers (Appears as / Is, with Anchor Item and Holy Weakness / Became this because); district; DREAD (comic / mixed / serious / mystery); how it frightens (what makes it scary without gore); where the comedy lives (in the people, not the creature); a one-session score seed (the problem and the hook); a choice with a human cost for the crew; one thread it can plant for a larger story (an Omen sign, a Subway rumour, a faction move, an NPC).
2. MIX: at least 10 genuinely frightening, 10 sad or tragic, 10 funny, and 10 mixed. At least 12 drawn from non-Western traditions, treated respectfully (do not treat living religious figures as jokes or villains; if a creature comes from a living faith, say so and adapt carefully).
3. MODERN LEGENDS: include at least 10 modern or urban-legend style (liminal spaces, elevators, vending machines, lost-in-the-mall stories, internet-era tales), grounded in real examples where possible.
4. COMPLICATIONS: 15 one-line Resonance complications for bad rolls (strange but not lethal), in the style of the existing generator.
5. RECOMMENDED ORDER: say which 10 are best for Seasons 1, 2 and 3 and why (early ones should be simple; late ones can be strange).
6. WARNINGS: flag anything that is too gory, too close to a real tragedy, or likely to be uncomfortable at a table of friends.

DELIVERABLES: a table per group (frightening / sad / funny / mixed / modern), then the complications list, the recommended order, and the warnings. Keep each creature to about 6 lines.

RULES
- Do NOT edit any files. Report only.
- Every folklore claim needs a source URL; mark every invented creature as INVENTED. Do not invent quotes or sources.
- Do not duplicate anything in 06-Scores/Score-Catalog.md or 10-Generators/Urban-Legends.md.
- No safety-tool pages; the GM never acts anything out; keep each score runnable by a GM reading live with an AI co-GM.
````
