# Gemini Bestiary Slips Prompt

**Why:** the Anchor Cover Rule (`10-Generators/Three-Layer-Monster.md`) says every disguised entity must state *what the Anchor is, where it is kept, and how its cover slips without it*. All 40 entries in `10-Generators/Bestiary.md` name an Anchor but none states the slip. Gemini drafts them; Claude verifies and applies.

````
ROLE
You are a monster designer for an industrial-occult noir campaign with Supernatural/Scooby comedy. You can read local files.

READ: 10-Generators/Bestiary.md (all 40 entries), 10-Generators/Three-Layer-Monster.md (the Anchor Cover Rule), 03-NPCs/Active/Merton.md and Mabel-Ashby.md (worked examples of a slip).

THE RULE
An Anchor is what keeps a hidden entity in the world and in its human cover. While it is whole and within the entity's reach it keeps its mundane cover (human shape, job, life); it need not be worn. Separate them and the cover slips in a way that fits the entity (examples: Merton changes to his true form and the Surge begins; Mabel loses cohesion and thins toward the wraith; Emmerich unravels over 1d6 hours). A seized Anchor also pings Apex scanners at 200 ft.

DO THIS
For EACH of the 40 entries, write one entry in this format, nothing else:
  #N Name | ANCHOR KEPT: where it is kept (one phrase) | SLIP: how the cover slips when the Anchor is taken or separated (what the crew SEES, one or two sentences, scary or sad, never a joke) | SPEED: instant / minutes / 1d6 hours | MENACE: any new danger in the slip
Keep each slip consistent with the entry's Three Layers, its Holy Weakness, its district, and the living-tradition respect rule (13 entries are drawn from living traditions: never make the spirit or the believers the joke; the slip must be dignified there).
Flag any entry where the existing Anchor is a poor fit for the slip (say what Anchor would work better).

RULES
- Do NOT edit any files. Report only.
- Do not invent lore about real cultures; mark anything you are not sure of as INVENTED.
- Nothing racist and nothing that mocks a culture or religion. Gore and violence are fine.
````
