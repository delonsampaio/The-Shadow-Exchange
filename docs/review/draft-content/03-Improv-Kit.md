## 5. If the table zigs
1. **They ignore the envelope.** 🔊 "The envelope is still on the counter the next day, and damper." Greta phones. If they still refuse, run a §2 rumour, and every faction clock ticks once.
2. **They want to board a train.** 🔊 "The platform is empty. The departure board flips, and flips, and settles on nothing." Nobody boards before Arc IV; give them Ysolde or Bram instead.
3. **They dig for the Subway.** 🔊 "Two feet past the cellar floor, the shovel is standing in water." The water table is one storey down. The Subway comes to them somewhere quiet and unwatched.
4. **They attack a PROTECTED NPC.** The NPC is gone a heartbeat early, or survives at a cost. Narrate it as a consequence (Heat +2, or Subway standing −1), then cut to the fallout.
5. **They go to the press.** Teddy or Rollo is the "journalist"; Hester Lyle arrives within a day. Run a cover-up score: who forgets?
6. **They befriend the target.** Good. Offer the Anchor choice early; Greta has a "Documented Relocation" form at half pay.
7. **They split up.** Cut between them each beat; rejoin via the Turnstile table.
8. **They chase a minor NPC all night.** That NPC overheard one of tonight's prep clues, and becomes the doorway.
9. **They take the Apex buyout early.** Run it: Kip becomes their handler and envelopes arrive on Apex letterhead. It is a valid path (check the Express Kit end states).
10. **They tell a civilian everything.** Winnie or Prudence hears it. ❓ ASK THE TABLE: "Do you want this to stick? The Ministry will hear." ⏸ REACTION STOP. If yes, Decay +1 at the next faction turn.

## 6. Cover-story twists
1. The NPC *also* works for your invented department and wants to talk shop.
2. The NPC is thrilled. They've waited years for someone to inspect this.
3. A real letter arrives at the shop next week, addressed to your fake department. It's from Greta.
4. Penhallow is standing right behind the NPC.
5. The NPC asks to see the badge. Whoever holds it describes the photo.
6. A rival inspector is already on site, from an even more absurd department.
7. It works too well, and you're booked for three more inspections.
8. A child believes it and follows you, taking notes.
9. The NPC is a Twice-Born, knows exactly what you are, and plays along to see what you'll do.
10. Drennick and Foss are in the lobby. They recognise the cover. Not *you*. Yet.

## 7. Run a score with no prep
1. **Target (d6):** 1 a Twice-Born whose Anchor pings scanners · 2 a Relic a civilian found · 3 a Biological in the wrong place · 4 an Echo bound to a routine · 5 a person (witness, hunter, mole) · 6 an item someone else stole. Build any creature with `10-Generators/Three-Layer-Monster.md`. No new species tonight.
2. **Where:** the rumour's district. Read one §1 scene on arrival.
3. **Engagement roll**, then a Detection clock (4) and one goal clock (4 or 6).
4. **Complication (d6):** 1 Apex is already there · 2 a civilian witness from the bench · 3 a rival firm holds the same envelope · 4 the target isn't who the envelope says · 5 the building is wrong (water, wiring, a locked floor) · 6 Greta arrives early.
5. **Twist (d6, halfway):** 1 the client lied · 2 the target is protecting someone · 3 the Anchor belongs to someone the crew knows · 4 a form that reads wrong (🔒 GM ONLY: the Mimic's paper trail) · 5 it pays well because it's bait · 6 the cat is involved.
6. **Choice with a cost:** two options, each saying what the crew gets and who it hurts. Surface the human cost before the payout. 🔴, no jokes.
7. **Out:** payoff (the Board's 4 Coin standard; 2 for Subway odd jobs); Heat (SRD: 2 standard, 4 loud; +1 in the Overhang); a Turnstile exit or the van; Zoltar's card.
**Time:** ~20 min per step from step 3; in 90 minutes, merge steps 4–5.
