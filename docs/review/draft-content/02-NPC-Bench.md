# NPC Bench — 30 Drop-Ins

**How to use:** these are GM-facing. Only the **SAY** line and the look may be read to the table. **Layers** (Appears as / Is / Became this because) and **Hook** are 🔒 GM ONLY. When one is used, Claude moves the card into a proper `03-NPCs/` file, adds them to the NPC index and logs them with `npc_add`. Names were checked against every existing NPC, faction, score and generator file.

**Tags:** 🎭 funny (8) · 🔴 serious (8) · 🕷 sinister (6) · ☕ warm/useful (8) · **↺ REPLACES** = a stand-in if a canon NPC dies or leaves (6).

## Old City

**1. Augustin Penhallow** 🎭 — owner of *Penhallow's Pre-Loved Pages*, the rival bookshop across the street. **Look:** a cardigan with leather elbows and binoculars "for birds". **Wants:** proof that C&C is a front, which he believes means a rival book cartel.
SAY (narrowed eyes, very sure): "Twenty years I've watched your door. Nobody buys that many lamps."
**Layers:** a nosy rival / a lonely widower who keeps accurate times / his wife's shop failed, and C&C survived. **Hook:** his notebook logs every late-night return the crew makes. That makes him a witness risk, or a perfect alibi if they befriend him.

**2. Marisol Quintero** ☕ — runs a 24-hour key-cutting and shoe-repair booth. **Look:** a pencil behind each ear and a pastry in progress. **Wants:** someone to finally pay her the full rate.
SAY (warm, mouth full): "Bring me a photo of the lock and a pastry. I'll do the rest."
**Layers:** a cobbler / the best lock-reader in the city, who works from photographs / her father cut keys for the Ministry and was never thanked. **Hook:** an Acquire Asset contact (keys and entry). Last spring she cut a copy of a key for a Stalls Holdings courier. Use it in Mid-Arc 1.

**3. Reverend Josiah Kemble** 🔴 — night chaplain at Old City Mercy Hospital. **Look:** a cardigan over his collar and a thermos of tea that is mostly sugar. **Wants:** one honest answer for the patient in Room 412.
SAY (soft, unhurried): "Room 412 asks me the same question every night. I don't have an answer that's good enough."
**Layers:** a chaplain / the only one who listens to the long-stay patients / he once signed a Ministry silence form and has regretted it for twenty years. **Hook:** the human door into S6 *The John Doe*. He will tell the crew things the hospital won't if they come back a second night.

## The Meridian

**4. Teddy Vasquez-Lim** 🎭 — junior hunter at the **Meridian Group**, with an influencer's habits. **Look:** a ring light clipped to a tactical vest. **Wants:** a viral "cryptid-core" post that somehow doesn't get her fired.
SAY (bright, phone already up): "Okay, quick, can you look haunted? It's for the grid."
**Layers:** a liability / genuinely talented at Hunt / her family thinks she works in marketing. **Hook:** this is the Meridian "contact inside" that canon leaves open. She doesn't know Apex owns 40% of her firm, but her analytics show a cluster of Apex accounts watching her. She is useful in MA5.

**5. Céleste Varga** 🕷 — concierge of "discreet acquisitions" for Meridian clients. **Look:** a camel coat, a silent phone and a perfectly neutral smile. **Wants:** Anchors, for collectors who like a story attached.
SAY (velvet, already pricing you): "Everything sentimental has a resale value. I simply arrive before the grief does."
**Layers:** a personal shopper / a broker of seized Anchors / she learned that people grieve faster than they appraise. **Hook:** her client list includes an Apex vice-president and a buyer from *The Trophy Auction* (Episode 9). She makes every Anchor decision more expensive to get right.

**6. Saoirse Dunmore** ☕ — reference librarian, Meridian Public Library. **Look:** reading glasses on a beaded chain and an "ASK ME" badge she means. **Wants:** to find out who has had *The Tidewater Almanac* out since 1961.
SAY (hushed, delighted): "Overdue since 1961. I've been dying to know who has it."
**Layers:** a librarian / the city's best Gather Information contact / she was the kid who read under the desk. **Hook:** she has noticed one borrower who renews the same volume every fourteen days, by post, on a card that cannot still be valid. This is a surface route to **Senna Calder**.

## The Fairground District

**7. Rollo Dunkirk** 🎭 — wears the costume of *Corny the Kernel*, the mascot of a popcorn chain. **Look:** a giant foam kernel; you never see his face. **Wants:** to be believed, once.
SAY (muffled, earnest): "I saw it. I was the kernel. Nobody listens to the kernel."
**Layers:** a mascot / the best-placed witness on the Fairground strip / people stopped looking at him years ago, so he sees everything. **Hook:** a witness for any Fairground score. The camera in his costume head recorded something he hasn't watched yet, which is a cover-up problem.

**8. Jasper Wilde** 🕷 — owner of *The Lantern Room*, one of the three Resonance-adjacent after-hours clubs. **Look:** a velvet jacket, too many rings and the smile of a man who knows the Ministry's thresholds exactly. **Wants:** to stay one notch under the scanners' alarm level.
SAY (smiling, a little too close): "Everybody down here's trying to feel something. I just charge at the door."
**Layers:** a club owner / he farms Resonance bleed as "atmosphere" / he grew up poor next to a haunted building and noticed that people paid to stand near it. **Hook:** his bouncer is a Twice-Born held by debt. Freeing him earns Favor and makes an enemy of a man with Apex friends.

**9. Mercy Adeyemi** 🔴 ↺ **REPLACES Harlan Okafor** — retired hunter who runs a 24-hour laundromat behind the Grand Theater, used as a Hunter Underground dead drop. **Look:** a prosthetic left hand and a cigarette she never lights. **Wants:** fewer young hunters on the Wall.
SAY (low, measured): "Wash is two dollars. Advice is free. Bad advice costs you a friend."
**Layers:** a laundromat owner / the Underground's other elder / she and Harlan worked one job in 1990 and haven't spoken since. **Hook:** she won't look at the Wall of the Missing. If Harlan falls, she takes the intel role (a +1 lead ahead of the Ministry list) without the diner.

## The Docks

**10. Roz Tuttle** 🎭 — shop steward for the dockworkers' union. **Look:** a hi-vis vest, a union pin and a clipboard of grievances. **Wants:** every monster sighting logged as overtime.
SAY (dead serious, pen ready): "If it's got more than four legs, it's time-and-a-half."
**Layers:** a union rep / she keeps the only honest record of Docks sightings / the company stopped paying hazard pay in 1998. **Hook:** her overtime claims form a map of Biological activity, a free clue for *Fisherman's Report*-type jobs and Mid-Arc 2.

**11. Tomasz Wrona** 🔴 — night foreman at a cold-storage company. **Look:** a frost-bitten beanie and two pairs of gloves. **Wants:** the truth about his brother.
SAY (flat, tired): "My brother went into Unit Six for a pallet. The paperwork says he quit."
**Layers:** a foreman / a grieving brother who stayed for the night shift so he could watch / the Ministry closed the case before the family was told. **Hook:** Unit Six is near the Apex black site. His brother's photo belongs on Harlan's Wall, and putting it there is a 🔴 scene.

**12. Dr. Femi Oduya** ☕ — a vet who runs an all-night animal clinic in the Docks and patches up hunters off the books. **Look:** scrubs covered in dog hair and reading glasses pushed up. **Wants:** her patients, all of them, to stop being so reckless.
SAY (brisk, kind): "Lie down. If you bark, I bill extra."
**Layers:** a vet / the city's Recover contact for anyone who can't go to a hospital / she failed her medical boards by one point and decided animals were kinder. **Hook:** she keeps a drawer of Anchors left behind by patients who never came back for them. If Gerald is kept, she is his vet.

**13. Yardley Pike** ☕ ↺ **REPLACES The Fence** — Docks pawnbroker who buys Relics above ground. **Look:** a jeweller's loupe, fingerless gloves and an eternal cold. **Wants:** stock that won't get him arrested.
SAY (flat, appraising): "I give you a third. I don't ask where it's from. I forget your face by lunch."
**Layers:** a pawnshop / a surface fence at worse rates, for crews with no Subway access / he lost his licence to the Ministry and never his contacts. **Hook:** he also buys from Apex Cleaners. One "unclaimed" item in his window belongs to a Twice-Born the crew knows.

## The Overhang

**14. Prudence Abernathy-Kask** 🎭 — captain (acting) of the Overhang Neighbourhood Watch. **Look:** a hi-vis vest over a dressing gown and a laminated lanyard. **Wants:** everyone to fill in her Suspicious Activity Log, which she designed herself, and which is better than the Ministry's.
SAY (bright, clipboard-proud): "I've logged your van eleven times. Twice it was parked beautifully."
**Layers:** a busybody / an accurate record of Resonance events with timestamps / she reported a burglary once and nobody came. **Hook:** any Overhang score gives a witness roll. Her log would be gold to the Ministry, to Apex or to the crew, whoever asks nicely first.

**15. Abel Mwangi** 🔴 — school crossing guard on Tannery Row, and a Twice-Born. **Look:** a reflective sash, a hand-painted STOP paddle and the patience of a century. **Wants:** to see the next generation across.
SAY (gentle, immovable): "I've crossed four generations on this street. I'll cross the next one, or I'll be standing here when they come."
**Layers:** a lollipop man / a Twice-Born, Anchor: his 1931 STOP paddle / he promised a drowned child's mother he would never leave the corner. **Hook:** Apex scan vans keep idling at his crossing. His address is on the list Harrow sells in MA5, and he is a natural person to shelter in S27.

**16. Agathe Tull** 🕷 — a Twice-Born landlady who owns three Overhang buildings. **Look:** a cardigan, a ring of forty keys and the softest voice in the district. **Wants:** quiet tenants, at any price.
SAY (sweet, the ledger already closed): "My tenants are very quiet. I make sure of it."
**Layers:** a kind old landlady / she sells nervous tenants to the Ministry to keep her own file closed / she was reported once, survived, and decided it would never be her again. **Hook:** three of her tenants have "moved out" this year. She is the Twice-Born who betrays Twice-Born, a moral test for the Favor system.

**17. Winnie Achterberg** ☕ ↺ **REPLACES Agnes Pell** — retired school cook who runs the Overhang's Tuesday bingo. **Look:** an apron over her Sunday best and a tray that is never empty. **Wants:** to feed the crew, who are "too thin, all three of you".
SAY (delighted, already pouring): "You're too thin, all three of you, and I heard about the thing on Fifth, so sit."
**Layers:** a feeder / she hears every rumour in the district by the second card / her son moved away and doesn't call. **Hook:** a warm rumour source. If Agnes is gone, Winnie inherits the penny dreadful (she reads it aloud at bingo, wrongly).

## The Ministry

**18. Fitch Ambrose** 🎭 — Requisitions officer, Old City field office. **Look:** sleeve garters and a stamp for every mood. **Wants:** the requisition process respected.
SAY (apologetic, final): "Your request for 'something for the ghost' has been approved as one flashlight. Batteries are a separate form."
**Layers:** a gatekeeper / a man who loves rules because they are fair / he once approved something without a form and someone died. **Hook:** his denial log shows what *other* firms requested for the same addresses. It is evidence of Kress triple-booking (*The Double-Booked Haunting*).

**19. Hester Lyle** 🔴 ↺ **REPLACES Deputy Director Reyes** *(as the inside ally, not the rank)* — a Memory Remediation nurse who does the "forgetting" for civilian witnesses. **Look:** a cardigan over Ministry grey and a pocket full of mints. **Wants:** to know each afternoon she takes out of a person was worth it.
SAY (quiet, exact): "I take one afternoon out of a person. I'd like to know it was worth it."
**Layers:** a nurse / the Ministry's conscience with clinic access / she wiped her own sister's memory under orders. **Hook:** she keeps a private list of every name she has wiped. One of them is a hunter whose photo is on Harlan's Wall.

**20. Lionel Sparrowe** 🕷 — an "observer" attached to the Suppression Bureau. **Look:** a grey suit, a pencil sharpened at both ends and a pleasant face you forget. **Wants:** numbers.
SAY (mild, writing): "Don't mind me. I'm only counting."
**Layers:** an auditor / a counter of every Twice-Born a firm lets walk / Kress promoted him after a single report. **Hook:** he is how Kress "notices" the crew (Trigger: protect a Twice-Born → noticed within two sessions). Seeing his face twice is the warning.

**21. Oswin Tabor** ☕ — night archivist, Ministry Records. **Look:** a cardigan with a pocket protector and crumbs. **Wants:** cake, and an audience for his indexing.
SAY (whispering, thrilled): "This file doesn't exist, so I can't lend it to you until Thursday."
**Layers:** an archivist / the best Ministry Gather Information contact if Greta won't bend / he trained under Dasha Lowe and still waters the plant on her desk. **Hook:** he'd help find her. He is also the slow route to Corvin Pryce's files (S17).

## Apex Aegis

**22. Kip Hollander** 🎭 ↺ **REPLACES Renwick Stalls** — Apex "Acquisitions Outreach" associate. **Look:** a quarter-zip, a lanyard and white teeth. **Wants:** the crew to accept a fruit basket. And the buyout.
SAY (relentless, sunny): "Before you say no, I want you to really sit with how nice this basket is."
**Layers:** a salesman / the friendly face Apex sends before the Cleaners / he's paying off his mother's hospital bill. **Hook:** squeezable. Feed him a compliment and he leaks the buyout's real number. If Stalls is gone, Kip is the shell-company face the crew can lean on.

**23. Dr. Imogen Halse** 🔴 — Apex research lead and designer of the Resonance Siphon. **Look:** a lab coat over running clothes, bitten nails and a precise voice. **Wants:** to believe her work saves lives.
SAY (clinical, then not): "The readings spike for about four seconds. I used to call that noise."
**Layers:** a scientist / a would-be defector / she found out what "calibration subject" means. **Hook:** she has Artie Bell's calibration logs (MA2). She could defect if the crew gives her somewhere to go.

**24. Holt Brannigan** 🕷 — Apex Cleaner squad lead. **Look:** a plain windbreaker, a wedding ring and a calm that doesn't change. **Wants:** a clean site and home by six.
SAY (pleasant, unblinking): "We're not here for you. We're here for the paperwork you're standing on."
**Layers:** a polite contractor / Apex's best Cleaner / he believes he prevents worse men from doing this job. **Hook:** he bought a book at C&C last month and paid cash. He has been inside the shop, and the receipt is in the till.

**25. Rhys Okonedo** ☕ — Apex surveillance drone pilot, aged 24. **Look:** a hoodie, a headset round his neck and energy-drink cans. **Wants:** out, and a reference.
SAY (whispering, headset half on): "I'm supposed to log you. My drone has a lot of battery issues near bookshops."
**Layers:** a drone jockey / a mole in waiting / he took the job for the student loan and saw the Docks footage. **Hook:** he can blind Apex surveillance for one score (Acquire Asset, quality 2). If he's caught, the Cleaners trigger.

## Rival firms and the Underground

**26. Delphine Arkwright** 🔴 — bookkeeper, **Harrow & Sons**. **Look:** sleeve protectors and a pencil worn to the stub. **Wants:** to keep eleven jobs alive.
SAY (exhausted, precise): "We have nine days of payroll and a grandfather who thinks pride is a currency."
**Layers:** a bookkeeper / the only one at Harrow who reads the bank statements / she owes the family her career and knows it. **Hook:** she has seen payments from a numbered company. It is the plant for the Harrow Patriarch's record sales (due by S12) and a witness in MA5.

## The Subway *(after the first visit only)*

**27. Pim Halvard** 🎭 — Subway notary, Mezzanine (her desk moves with the Whisperer's). **Look:** half-moon spectacles, a brass seal and a ledger of promises. **Wants:** everything in writing, witnessed.
SAY (dry, stamping): "Sign here, here, and here. The thumbprint is optional. The sincerity is not."
**Layers:** a clerk / the Subway's notary, whose seal settles disputes before Officer Bram does / she was a city clerk who wouldn't stop filing after she died. **Hook:** she holds Harrow & Sons' old notarized promise never to hunt the Quiet Platform. Breaking it is an MA5 lever.

**28. Ysolde Marr** 🔴 — the lamplighter who replaces bulbs across the platforms. **Look:** a long ladder, a canvas bag of bulbs and a face lit from below. **Wants:** every platform lit.
SAY (low, counting): "Forty-one bulbs on Platform 2 last year. Thirty-eight now. I don't replace what doesn't come back."
**Layers:** maintenance / a living Decay barometer / she took the job so that nobody would ever be left in the dark again. **Hook:** use her count to show Decay *before* the number moves (Living Subway §8: change the place first). She will not go near the Yellow Line.

**29. Mr. Saltonstall** 🕷 — a ticket inspector who appears on the Main Hall concourse, a Twice-Born. **Look:** a 1920s inspector's cap, a brass punch and a book of faces. **Wants:** to see your ticket. Nobody has a ticket.
SAY (polite, patient): "Tickets, please. No? That's quite all right. I'll remember your face instead."
**Layers:** a harmless relic / the last inspector of a private tram line that closed in 1927, Anchor: his ticket punch / no one told him the line had closed. **Hook:** he remembers every face, and he sells those memories to the Whisperer. Seeing him means someone now has the crew's faces. He never explains tickets or trains.

**30. Marigold Oyelaran** ☕ ↺ **REPLACES Dev (The Last Stop)** — Twice-Born who runs a soup cart on Platform 1, *The Second Helping*. **Look:** a headscarf, a ladle and a steam that smells of someone's grandmother. **Wants:** nobody leaves hungry.
SAY (warm, ladle up): "Soup's free if you're sad. You look sad. Have two."
**Layers:** a soup lady / a Twice-Born, Anchor: a dented 1960s tin ladle / she cooked for a shelter that burned and kept cooking. **Hook:** she clears 1 Stress or gives one rumour per visit (Dev's service). Her best recipe came from a cook who went Past the Yellow Line.
