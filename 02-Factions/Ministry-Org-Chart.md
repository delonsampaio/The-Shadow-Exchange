## What each branch does to the crew
| Branch | The crew sees | Mechanical hooks |
|---|---|---|
| **Greta (Compliance)** | Forms, follow-up questions about the square footage | Favour = reduce Heat; the Audit clock's visible face (Form 44-C, S4+); 47-B/22-A/31 forms; she CANNOT leave before Season 2 |
| **Vance (Compliance)** | A name on letters; stern; a compliance team when Audit hits 4 | Locks C&C's Vault/Cold Storage if Audit fills; hinge at S19+ (a coup) |
| **Reyes (Oversight)** | "A day more", a clearer brief | Routes better contracts; helps if shown Apex corruption (carefully, slowly); HINGE ONLY (S27–28) |
| **Kress (Suppression)** | Nothing in S1–S6; a letterhead at S7 | Reroutes and manufactures contracts; freezes the licence at S12; falls or goes rogue at S28 |
| **Thorne / Dane (liaison)** | The friendly field contact | Thorne's tells (S1–S15); Dane is a stiff replacement (S16) |
| **Couriers** | Envelopes | Deliver bounties; carry Form 44-C after S4 |

## Reporting lines in play
- Day to day: **PCs → Greta → Vance**. Greta cannot overrule Kress's routing; she can only file a form about it.
- Contracts: the envelope is routed **by Oversight (Reyes) or manufactured by Suppression (Kress)**; the crew cannot tell which. A job that "sweeps" Apex is a Kress routing.
- Audit: **Compliance** inspectors tick it; the Audit clock is **GM-secret until S4**. Kress's office **feeds** it only through "manufactured compliance flags".
- Entanglements that are Ministry (Usual Suspects, Questioning, Interrogation, Arrest, Show of Force by a sweep) tick the Audit silently.
