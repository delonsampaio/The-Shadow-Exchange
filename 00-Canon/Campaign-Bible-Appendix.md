# Campaign Bible — Appendix (restored from v4)

The v5 bible kept the axioms but dropped working mechanics that other files still lean on. They live here now.

---

## Lair Mechanics

| Fixture | Mechanical effect |
|---|---|
| **Trophy Wall** | +1d on Study for a monster type the crew has hunted before, taken before a score. Automatic Heat source if the Ministry raids the lair. |
| **Hunter's Journal** | +1d Study for any PC who consults it before a score involving a known monster type. Lost if the Ministry confiscates it. |
| **Scanner Station** | Bank of modified shortwave radios (intercepts Ministry bounty traffic, picks up Harlan's-region AM) **plus** a Resonance detector — a single green lamp, always lit, reach ≈ two blocks. Tinker for a specific signature; Survey for general detection. |
| **Cold Storage Locker** | Pauses Biological Spoilage. |
| **Lead-Lined Vault** | Items stored here emit no Resonance; scanners see nothing. |
| **Lair benefits** | +1 Tier for crafting/preserving Quality. *Hardened Heart:* reduced Heat from supernatural messes. |

## Null-Tech (Apex Aegis)
- **Cold Iron Rounds:** +1 Harm to supernatural targets; prevent shifting for the scene.
- **Siphons:** suppress a Relic's power for the scene. Siphoned items sell at half at The Fence. A Tinker roll in Downtime may restore; failure is permanent.
- **Anchor Scanners:** detect Anchor Item Resonance within **200 ft**. Carrying a seized Anchor near Apex = flagged immediately.
- **The Cleaner Protocol:** at crew Heat 6+ (or Apex standing ≤ −2), Cleaners are assigned to disrupt scores and damage the shop.

## Loot Catalogue
- **Biologicals** (perishable; 3-seg Spoilage Clock, ticks each Downtime; ticks twice per scene on Away Jobs): Ethereal/Barghest Adrenal Gland, Vampire Blood (healing/life extension), Gorgon Scalp (temporary petrification), Phase-Shifter Heart (phase through one wall per use).
- **Relics** (active, cursed, never stop emitting Resonance): The Monkey's Paw (auto-crit once; triggers Tragedy Clock, GM-secret), The Mourning Veil (invisible to the living, visible to ghosts), Frequency Radio (mind-read 50 ft, 1 Stress/min), **The Ghost Map** (inks itself to show active Subway manifestation points — mid-game MacGuffin), **The Casting Mask** (unique; do not let players obtain it easily).
- **Anchor Items**: one per disguised monster; seizing starts the 1d6-hour clock. *Pre-planned Anchors:* a silver locket (librarian — Senna's card is her version), a brass compass pointing to one alley (former sailor; the Job Board posting), a laminated child's drawing (hunted Twice-Born in hiding), a coin from no country (recent arrival; hums at 3 AM; something larger is looking for it).

## Clock Staging (don't dump them all)
| When | Clock | How |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions 1–3 | **Detection** (4) | The only clock the crew sees |
| Session 4 | **Biological Spoilage** (3) | Their first Biological starts to rot between sessions |
| Session 6 | **Subway Decay** (6) | They walk in and see it inked on the transit map |
| Mid-game | **Tragedy** (4, secret) | Only if the Monkey's Paw is used |
| From S2 | **Omen** (8, secret) | Symptoms only (see `Active-Clocks.md`) |
| Late | **Crossroads Cost** | Arrives as a consequence, never as a system |

*Exception:* the **Ministry Audit** (4) can appear as soon as Double Bookkeeping fails — explain it as a consequence, not a rule.

## Away Job Rules
Out of town, no lair access: Detection Clock **3** segments; Spoilage ticks **twice** per scene; **Load −2**; **no Subway access**; **Harlan's Bonus** on completion (extra Coin + Hunter Underground rep).

## Urban Legends as Job Sources — The Ritual
Each Downtime a PC can read small-town papers / late-night AM / police blotters: roll **Attune or Study** → 1–3 leads of varying urgency (`10-Generators/Urban-Legends.md`). Three incidents in one district = something is nesting.

## Cursed Heirlooms — starter examples
| Heirloom | Wrong thing | How it gets worse |
|---|---|---|
| Pocket watch | Stopped at the moment someone died; ticks backward | Hands speed toward something |
| Photograph | Faces erase, one per month | Background changes to somewhere the crew will be |
| Personal journal | Language they don't speak but understand | New entries in their handwriting, dated in the future |
| Piece of clothing | Always clean | Smells of someone they thought was gone |

*(Degradation patterns: `Heirloom-Degradation-Patterns.md`.)*

## Three-Layer Rule — canonical vocabulary
The **Bible** uses *Holy Weakness / Mundane Cover / Tragic Angle*. The **Core Instructions and Generators** use *Mundane Cover / True Nature / Tragic Angle*. They are the same rule: **True Nature includes its Holy Weakness.** Write all four, name them either way.

## Tone Source
The v4 bible names its inspiration directly: **Supernatural** seasons 1–3 — road-worn, morally complicated, sometimes tragic — with Harvelle's Roadhouse (→ Harlan's Diner) and the civilian dragged in (→ Mara Halliday). The comedy register comes from the same show, plus *Scooby-Doo* (see `Comedy-Playbook.md`).
