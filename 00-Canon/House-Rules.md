# House Rules

This page ranks **second** in the priority order, right after `Session-Boot.md` (see its priority line); it beats Comedy-Playbook, Core and every prep file for rules and table procedure. The SRD wins for everything it covers that is not listed here.

|---|---|---|

## B. Who rolls
Players roll every action and resistance roll. **The GM never rolls against the players.** The exceptions are the dice the SRD gives the GM: the **Entanglement**, fortune rolls for NPC and clock outcomes (including the Broker's d6, where the Broker states the *kind* of cost, never the extent). The Co-GM never "rolls" in the fiction; it asks the GM or a player to roll.

## C. Table procedure
1. **Ten-minute envelope.** The contract card is on the TV within ten minutes of the start.
2. **Two-minute decision clock.** After five minutes of planning the Co-GM offers three options; at minute eight the GM reads: "Two minutes, then we go with whatever's on top."
3. **Every PC rolls in the key scene.** Point the next complication at whoever has rolled least.
4. **One big reveal per session.** A second reveal becomes next week's cold open.
5. **Cover-up first.** When a consequence is needed and nobody wants harm, use exposure (a witness, a phone, a form) before injury.
6. **Rules lookups take 60 seconds.** Otherwise the GM rules, the Co-GM logs it, and it is checked against the SRD afterwards.
7. **Hard stop at the agreed time.** If a score runs late, offer "cliffhanger here, or a five-minute fast-forward?" Never run more than 15 minutes over without the table agreeing.
8. **One table-invented detail per session goes into canon**, logged at once.
9. **Short session (90 minutes).** Open on the engagement roll, run one score, run downtime as a two-minute "text-message" round (one sentence per activity), recap by TV card.
10. **Absent player.** Their PC is on the job but offscreen (or "on another call"). They earn the crew's XP and half of their own triggers; others may only **Assist** for them; Heirloom and spotlight beats are held back.

## D. Canon protections the Co-GM always keeps *(🔒 GM ONLY: do not copy to the players' site)*
- No boarding a Subway train before Arc IV (one pass-by only). No digging below one storey. The Subway has no fixed address.
- Protected NPCs bend, they don't break: the fiction stops the table, narrated as a consequence, never as a refusal.
- Menace Rule: no comic sting while a real threat is on screen; monsters get no punchlines (the joke belongs to a person).
- The GM never acts anything out; they read labelled lines.

## D2. Content
Anything goes: gore, graphic violence, cursing, horror and grief are all fine for this table (private, adult, 20+ years of TTRPG experience). The only hard line: **nothing racist, and nothing that mocks a culture or a religion.** Monsters stay scary (Menace Rule). Do not add content warnings or soften scenes unless the GM asks.

## E. If a PC dies or a player leaves *(decide now, use later)*
- **Dead PC:** their Heirloom goes to the vault (or Wim, or another PC if the table wants); their photo goes on Harlan's Wall; the next PC arrives as a hunter from the Wall, a rival firm's survivor, or a Twice-Born asking for work. Friend and Rival NPCs carry over.
- **Player leaves:** the PC retires on screen or becomes a contact or cohort. For a crew of two: one fewer Detection tick per score; re-deal the climax-anchor PC rotation. Check the Express Ending Kit's "Before the credits" scenes.
