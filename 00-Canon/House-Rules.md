### A2. Rules we follow exactly (NOT deviations; nothing is skipped in early sessions)
*A mechanic the Ladder has not yet taught is "deferred", not banned: if a player asks, explain it in one line and use it.*
Each of these works as the official rules say, **from Session 1** (Session 0 has no score). Each is taught at its first use.
- **Heat and Wanted:** exactly the SRD. Heat is the track the players see. At **Heat 9** the crew gains **+1 Wanted level** and Heat **clears** (excess rolls over). **Wanted level sets the dice** rolled on the Entanglement roll (dice = Wanted level; **at Wanted 0 roll two dice and keep the lowest**); **Heat sets the result column** (0–3 / 4–5 / 6+). Wanted is on the crew sheet (`wanted:`); the only way to lower it is incarceration. *(An older note said Heat replaced Wanted; that was wrong and is removed.)*
- **Crew creation (S0):** the SRD exactly, with a weak hold and 0 Coin: two chosen upgrades (plus the Containment Suite fixtures), hunting grounds (default Old City, −1 status with that district's rival firm, preferred operation Extraction), two faction reactions (**+1 pleased, −2 annoyed**; the 1-Coin adjustments are unavailable at 0 Coin; the annoyed side is always a weak rival firm), and a favourite contact (+1 / −1).
- **Acquire Asset (S3):** the SRD roll; in S3 the first one is a free practice roll before the score (a teaching choice, not a rule change).
- **Entanglement:** rolled by the GM **after every score, from the first one (S1)**, after payoff and Heat (`07-Clocks/Heat-and-Entanglements.md` §2).
- **XP:** Desperate-roll XP **and the end-of-session XP tally and advancement happen every session from S1** (`00-Canon/End-of-Session-Ritual.md`). The Teaching Ladder teaches the tally at the end of S1 and advancement when a track first fills.

## B. Who rolls
Players roll every action and resistance roll. **The GM never rolls against the players.** The exceptions are the dice the SRD gives the GM: the **Entanglement**, fortune rolls for NPC and clock outcomes (including the Broker's d6, where the Broker states the *kind* of cost, never the extent). The Co-GM never "rolls" in the fiction; it asks the GM or a player to roll.

## C. Table procedure
1. **Ten-minute envelope.** The contract card is on the TV within ten minutes of the start.
2. **Two-minute decision clock.** After five minutes of planning the Co-GM offers three options; at minute eight the GM reads: "Two minutes, then we go with whatever's on top."
3. **Every PC rolls in the key scene.** Point the next complication at whoever has rolled least.
4. **One big reveal per session.** A second reveal becomes the next session's cold open.
5. **Cover-up first.** When a consequence is needed and nobody wants harm, use exposure (a witness, a phone, a form) before injury.
6. **Rules lookups take 60 seconds.** Otherwise the GM rules, the Co-GM logs it, and it is checked against the SRD afterwards.
7. **Hard stop at the agreed time.** If the evening is ending, stop at a safe stop and run the Pause (item 9). Never fast-forward. Never run more than 15 minutes over without the table agreeing.
8. **One table-invented detail per session goes into canon**, logged at once.
9. **Short night.** Nothing is cut: play to the next safe stop in the prep, run the **Pause** (a "To be continued" card, one recap line, a Night Log row; no Zoltar, XP, retrospective or Faction Turn until the session is finished), and resume next sitting with a "Previously" recap card.
10. **Absent player.** Their PC is on the job but offscreen (or "on another call"). They earn the crew's XP and half of their own triggers (counted per session, not per sitting); others may only **Assist** for them; Heirloom and spotlight beats are held back.

## D. Canon protections the Co-GM always keeps *(🔒 GM ONLY: do not copy to the players' site)*
- No boarding a Subway train before Arc IV (one pass-by only). No digging below one storey. The Subway has no fixed address.
- **Season 1 numbers:** Decay stays ≤ 3 (first Decay 4 ≈ S41; Omen 5 ≈ S41). The **Ministry Audit clock is GM-secret until S4** (players see nothing of it before then).
- **Anchor Cover Rule:** every disguised entity keeps its human cover only while its Anchor is intact and present; see `10-Generators/Three-Layer-Monster.md` and the Anchor line in each Bestiary entry.
- **Load and Kit:** Load is taught in S1 paired with Flashback (`01-Crew/Hunter-Kit.md`); anything a PC uses was shown, packed or a Flashback.
- Protected NPCs bend, they don't break: the fiction stops the table, narrated as a consequence, never as a refusal.
- Menace Rule: no comic sting while a real threat is on screen; monsters get no punchlines (the joke belongs to a person).
- The GM never acts anything out; they read labelled lines.

## D2. Content
Anything goes: gore, graphic violence, cursing, horror and grief are all fine for this table (private, adult, 20+ years of TTRPG experience). The only hard line: **nothing racist, and nothing that mocks a culture or a religion.** Monsters stay scary (Menace Rule). Do not add content warnings or soften scenes unless the GM asks.

## E. If a PC dies or a player leaves *(decide now, use later)*
- **Dead PC:** their Heirloom goes to the vault (or Wim, or another PC if the table wants); their photo goes on Harlan's Wall; the next PC arrives as a hunter from the Wall, a rival firm's survivor, or a Twice-Born asking for work. Friend and Rival NPCs carry over.
- **Player leaves:** the PC retires on screen or becomes a contact or cohort. For a crew of two: one fewer Detection tick per score; re-deal the climax-anchor PC rotation. Check the Express Ending Kit's "Before the credits" scenes.
