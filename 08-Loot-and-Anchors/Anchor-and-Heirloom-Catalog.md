# Anchor & Heirloom Catalog

**Rules used:** seizing an Anchor collapses the disguise in 1d6 h; seized Anchors are Tier 1 Contraband (`Campaign-Bible.md`). Twice-Born mapping: Enmity −1 · Ambivalence 0 · Favor +1 and a token, max 3 (`Faction-Tracker.md`, `The-Twice-Born.md`). Senna and Aldric refuse crews that destroy Anchors without exhausting alternatives (`Senna-Calder.md`, `Aldric-Voss.md`). Access to the Subway can *fade* for a crew that destroys Anchors with no exception (`Overview.md`). **PROPOSAL** = invented here.

**Source key:** Bible = `Campaign-Bible.md` · App = `Campaign-Bible-Appendix.md` · S1–S3 = `Session-0N-Prep.md` · SC = `Score-Catalog.md` · S1P = `Season-1-Plan.md` · UL = `Urban-Legends.md` · LS = `Living-Subway.md` · HDP = `Heirloom-Degradation-Patterns.md`.
