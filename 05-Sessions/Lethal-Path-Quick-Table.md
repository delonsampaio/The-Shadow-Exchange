# Lethal-Path Quick Table (GM ONLY)

Anything goes: gore, violence and cursing are allowed. **No jokes while harm is on screen** (Menace Rule). Nothing is softened; the cost is consequence, not a refusal.

## The rules that never change
1. **Offer Resistance first** through S3 (House-Rules §A); from S4 remind once and let the players call for it. A Flashback if they want one.
2. **Offer the other doors once**, in the fiction, not as a lecture (*"He's stopped swinging. He's looking at the photograph."*). Then let the table decide.
3. **Harm ladder:** Level 1 hampers, Level 2 takes a die, Level 3 needs help, **Level 4 = catastrophic, permanent or fatal (SRD).** Offer Resistance first.
4. **A kill = +2 Heat** (not +4; it never stacks with another Heat source for the same act). *Death Veil* (a crew special ability from Session 0) removes this.
5. **Witnesses:** a civilian who sees it = Detection +2 and a witness who remembers. Agnes, Gerald, Bernard hear things: they react later.
6. **Twice-Born standing −1** when the victim is Twice-Born or the Anchor owner; the Subway reads it.
7. **Protected NPCs bend, they do not break.** If a PC attacks Greta, Harlan, Reyes, Thorne, Kress or any 🔒 protected NPC: the NPC is gone a heartbeat early or survives at a cost; narrate it as a consequence (Heat +2, or Subway standing −1), then cut to the fallout.
8. **After a kill, the ledger:** log Heat, Detection, standing and the Anchor outcome (`Live-Ledger.md`, `Anchor-Ledger.md`).
