# Season 1 Plan — "The Paper & The Mask"

A weekly table (2–3 h, past campaigns 2–3 years) needs more than the 13-session spine in `Campaign-Arc.md`. This plan treats the campaign as a TV show with **one endgame** (the Waking) and three seasons. Season 1 is Sessions 0–28: the pilot (S0–3, already written), then six **mid arcs** (3–5 sessions) interleaved with five **mini arcs** (1–2 sessions) and player-chosen **episode slots**. Everything below is ADOPTED (2026-11-20); individual arc contents remain proposals to refine in play. Every arc ends in a **choice with a cost**, not a boss fight, and every arc names its **endgame link**.

## 0. Decisions (made 2026-11-20)
1. **Length — APPROVED by the GM:** 80 sessions in three seasons, with the Waking as the series finale.
2. **Unmasking moved to Session 15 — ADOPTED.** Three optional extra tells added (7–9, S9–S14) so the table can catch it three independent ways. Written into `Campaign-Arc.md` §5 and `Elias-Thorne.md`.
3. **Omen pacing — ADOPTED:** 2/8 by S3, hold until ~S11 (3/8), 4/8 at S25 (Aldric's broadcast stops), 5/8 at ~S41 (Apex finds Harlan's diner), 6/8 end of Season 2, 7 at ~S67, 8/8 only at the Waking. Written into `07-Clocks/Active-Clocks.md`.
4. **Who may die or leave — ADOPTED (defaults in §4).** Harlan's diner burns around S41 (mid-season finale of Season 2); whether Harlan dies is decided by the table's choices. Emery Vantage is dead by S10–11. Greta, PTOLEMY, the Broker, the Conductor, Zoltar, Consequence, the Architect and Thorne stay protected as listed.
5. **The Broker's price rule — ADOPTED:** the GM rolls the d6 secretly before the offer; the Broker states the *kind* of price (one of six) up front, never the extent. Written into `Crossroads-Broker.md`, `Stations.md`, `Campaign-Bible.md`.
6. **Real Elias Thorne is a cold true-believer — KEPT** (Mid-Arc 4).
7. **Season 1 finale (S28) — KEPT:** the single allowed pass-by train carries the face from Harlan's Wall photo ("HE WENT BACK", `Harlan-Okafor.md`).

## 3. Mid arcs and mini arcs (each ends in a choice with a cost)
**MID-ARCS**
1. **The Paper Pipeline (S4–7).** *Who is skimming the contracts before hunters see them?* Stalls is a terrified patsy; his binder carries Kress's letterhead *and* an appraisal of Curios & Chronicles marked "LEASEHOLDER UNVERIFIABLE — HOLD." **Choice:** hand Stalls to Reyes (trust, pauses Apex's play, Stalls vanishes into custody, Apex standing −1) *or* extort him for 4 Coin (fills the lair's 4-Coin cap and saves toward Tier I's 8 Coin; Kress unchecked). *Endgame:* Decay goes live (1/6); Truth Table: Stalls = Apex, Kress's name, the word "Architect".
2. **Docks & Daughters (S8–11).** *Dig up the proof or save the people still breathing?* Emery Vantage is dead; Dasha Lowe is alive indexing records for Apex; the "hunted Tier-1 Biological" is **Artie Bell**, a Twice-Born kept siphoned to calibrate scanners (his Anchor: a child's laminated drawing of a bus, taped inside the scanner cage; his daughter drew it). **Choice:** take Kress's contract chain + Dasha and leave Artie (proof, Twice-Born standing −1) *or* free Artie too (+1 Twice-Born Favor, but the evidence room burns and Kress learns C&C's name). Dasha also remembers the originating form number — a second route to why Emmerich was targeted, so freeing Artie never costs the clue. *Endgame:* Decay 2, Omen 3, Apex goes hot.
3. **The Squeeze & The Mask (S12–15).** *Is it a friend helping you, or a hand guiding you in?* Kress freezes the license and Apex's Regional Director offers a buyout (S12: a squeeze and a lifeline together); the Broker job and the Ghost Map shard are the S13 B-plot; Thorne steps up as the lifeline; the S14 job collapses (nothing else happens in S14). **Twist:** Thorne is the Understudied and the S14 ambush was a staged exposure. **Choice:** hand the Understudied and the Casting Mask to Vance/Reyes (clears the license, but the Mask sits where Kress has clearance) *or* let it go in a truce for real Thorne's address and Kress's exploit ledger (Ministry license suspended for one arc; the fox is out). Either way the coastal job arrives (a Harlan's Bonus contract): with the address the crew arrives first (Controlled); without it they go in blind (Risky). *Endgame:* the Mimic thread's big turn; Thorne leaves the show.
4. **The Ghosts of Faces Worn (S16–19).** *If you give someone back the life that was stolen, what do you do when you don't like who they are?* A coastal Away Job (the culprit is caught by a trap that backfires — no mask-pull) hears of a man on the coast who says he is Elias Thorne; the crew meets him at S18. He turns out to be a rigid true-believer: *"If I get my badge back I will prosecute Kress first and your firm second."* Tamsin Solano's old survey notes show the Subway once kept appearing over an impossible dry pocket. **Choice:** put real Thorne before Vance (Kress exposed; a hardliner auditing the crew) *or* give him and Tamsin new lives (the black market is safe; Vance won't move on Kress). *Endgame:* Omen stays 3; Architect clue #2.
5. **The Meridian Split (S20–23).** *Can small flawed people beat a monopoly without becoming it?* Apex owns 40% of Meridian Group; the Harrow Patriarch has been selling hunting records to Apex. **Choice:** expose him (saves the Castellan hunter, +1 Favor, Harrow & Sons folds) *or* bury it for Harrow's list of the Twice-Born addresses Apex holds (used at S27) plus 4 Coin (Harrow survives compromised; the crew lies to Ottilie). *Endgame:* Ministry/Apex pressure re-ordered.
6. **The Red Ink & The Pass-By (S24–28).** *If the thing waking under the city doesn't care about the Ministry or Apex, who holds the line?* Wim holds the lease; PTOLEMY's ledger is dated three days before Session 2; Aldric cuts his own broadcast because *"it"* was using his signal; Kress's rig amplifies the Omen. **Choice:** hand Kress's rig and records to Reyes (Kress falls; Apex's director escapes with half the Ghost Map) *or* overload the rig with the Ghost Map shard (or, if the shard was lost, a vault Relic or Aldric's dial) (Apex blinded, Decay holds at 3, Twice-Born Favor −1, Kress goes rogue). *Endgame:* Season 1 baseline: Decay 3, Omen 4.

**MINI-ARCS (1–2 sessions each, drop in anywhere in the flex window)**
- **A. The Tailor's Second Stitch** *(S5–9; needs Emmerich alive)* — someone is hunting a coat with a live Anchor thread. **Choice:** hide Emmerich in the Subway (he names the Understudied; his shop closes) *or* burn the thread to spoof the proxy.
- **B. Thursday's Soup & The Penny Dreadful** *(S6–13)* — Agnes's serial gets one detail dead right. **Adapted twist:** the writer's source is **Mara Halliday, unknowingly** (a rumour she repeated), so her corruption clock gets a push without making her a schemer. **Choice:** pulp the print run (the cover holds; Mara ticks +1) *or* buy it and tell Mara one honest truth.
- **C. The Compass & The Glass Mirrors** *(S9–14)* — Urban Legend 1 plus Subway Job Board #1; the mirrors show dry corridors beneath the Meridian's water table. **Choice:** return the compass to **Callum Oates** (+1 Favor) *or* collapse the bleed (his Anchor unravels).
- **D. Good Boy, Bad Press** *(S11–18; needs Gerald kept)* — Pruitt's "lost dog" flyer and a survey spike in the brick. **Choice:** fake Gerald's death on Ministry paper (Audit +1) *or* leak Pruitt's bribe (Heat +2).
- **E. The Coin From No Country** *(S17–23)* — Urban Legend 3 plus the unplaced "recent arrival," **Nadia Kestrel**. **Adapted:** the coin is **not a fare token** (the Subway has no tokens, and trains stay unexplained until Arc IV); it is an unexplained Omen-linked relic. **Choice:** seal it in the vault (the crew is marked; Nadia owes a Favor) *or* give it to PTOLEMY to pay her old debt (Echo vanishes; Nadia must disappear underground).

## 6. Ten new episodes (one session each; Three-Layer rule; every one plants a thread)
1. **The 11:47 Bus** — a night-bus driver who is an Echo bound to a 1978 schedule. *Choice:* retire the route or reroute it to the Docks. *Plants:* the destination roll flips to `LINE 0 — EXPRESS`.
2. **The Taxidermist's Choir** — a cricket-like colony nests in mounted birds and sings a dead wife's scales. *Choice:* harvest the vocal sacs or remove only the queen. *Plants:* a bird repeats a line from Aldric's unreleased warning.
3. **The Unclaimed Locker at Pier 9** — an iron-moth artisan sealed in since a 1982 strike. *Choice:* give his union pin to the Ministry or bring him to Cosma. *Plants:* Corvin Pryce's badge number scratched inside.
4. **The Velvet Booth at The Starlight** — a memory-siren lounge singer feeding on nostalgia. *Choice:* seize her record or broker a willing-patron quota. *Plants:* a 1969 photo of Harlan's lost crew.
5. **The Sub-Zero Intern** — a frost-carrier accidentally working for an Apex shell. *Choice:* extract her or keep her as a mole. *Plants:* a memo listing three Overhang blocks for Apex sweeps.
6. **The Wrong Funeral** — grief-hounds hold a funeral for a forgotten hunter; the crew exits a Subway turnstile into the cemetery. *Choice:* take a sample for the Fence or name the dead man on Harlan's Wall. *Plants:* an envelope addressed to C&C, postmarked six months before the crew formed.
7. **The Elevator That Goes to Basement Two** — a space-mimic mimicking a dry basement in a city where none can exist. *Choice:* kill it or evacuate its lost luggage via Wim. *Plants:* a luggage tag reading `PTOLEMY — PROPERTY OF LINE 0`.
8. **The Neighbor's Generator** — a retired tinker shields his granddaughter from scanners with a washing-machine dampener. *Choice:* confiscate it or rebuild it quietly. *Plants:* his oscilloscope shows an underground pulse closer by six feet a month.
9. **The Trophy Auction** *(Away Job)* — a crooked retired hunter fences real Anchors. *Choice:* free the trapped wraith or report the ballroom to Vance. *Plants:* a bidder texting photos of the bookmobile to Valerie Crane.
10. **The Double-Booked Haunting** — three firms get the same envelope; Kress triple-booked them. *Choice:* sabotage the rivals or call a truce. *Plants:* proof of Kress's divide-and-conquer routing.

## 7. Claude's design notes (what the plan still needs)
- **Tell spacing.** Moving the unmasking to S15 stretches the six Thorne tells over twelve sessions; (done: optional tells 7–9 are in `Campaign-Arc.md` §5) and design **three independent routes** to the truth (tells, Emmerich's name, the Casting Mask's file trail) so a quiet table cannot miss it.
- **One big reveal per session.** Where the pacing map stacks a twist, a reveal and a death in four sessions (Mid-Arc 3), spread them so each lands.
- **Spotlight.** Three PC spotlights in 28 sessions is thin; add a second per PC in Season 1B and rotate who anchors each mid-arc climax.
- **Blades math is not verified.** Tier, Rep, upgrade and Heat costs named in Gemini's cards were not checked against `Blades-in-the-Dark-SRD.md`; verify each before it goes into a prep file.
- **Reorderability.** Every arc should survive losing a session to sickness or travel; keep the "what do you want more of?" rule.
- **Crew progression (SRD):** a crew starts at strong hold with 2 Coin (this campaign deliberately starts Weak hold, 0 Coin — a house rule). Each score earns 2 Rep (+1 per Tier of the target above the crew). 12 Rep makes a weak hold strong; the next 12 Rep plus **new Tier × 8 Coin** raises the Tier. Targets: **Tier I around S12–14, Tier II around S26–28**. Upgrades come only from crew advancement; a cohort costs two upgrades.
- **Heirloom pacing:** stage 2 by ~S15, stage 3 by ~S41, stage 4 at ~S67; mid-arc beats are symptoms within a stage.
- **Aldric on screen:** guarantee three contacts before S25 (Scanner Station AM fragments from S4, the S9 Radio Signal episode, one Subway visit) so his silence hurts.
- **Plants to schedule:** Mara Reappearance Stage 2 (Intent) by S12 (the Corruption Clock only starts at Stage 4); the Harrow Patriarch's record sale mentioned in passing by S12; Mini-Arc B is optional.
- **Climax anchor PC:** rotate A, B, C, A, B, C across the climaxes at S7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 28, each keyed to that PC's Session 0 Friend or Rival.
- **Menace rule.** The monsters are meant to be scary. The comedy lives in the people, the bureaucracy and the absurd setups, never in the threat itself; see `Comedy-Playbook.md`.
- **Fronts.** Factions move between sessions through `Faction-Tracker.md`; the Omen and Decay tick on fiction, so a slow table gets a faster world, not a longer wait.
