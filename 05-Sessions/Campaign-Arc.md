# Campaign Arc — Sessions 0–80 (three seasons)

> **GM-PRIVATE.** This is the connective tissue. Sessions 0–3 are fully prepped. Sessions 4+ are *situations*, not scripts — plan the situation, not the story. Each entry lists what it plants, what it pays off, and the trigger conditions that move the threads.

**Terminology (fixed):** *Session* = one night at the table. *Score* = the job inside it (the original bible called these "Jobs"/"Heists"; those labels map to scores below). *Downtime* = the phase after.

---

## 1. The Spine (one paragraph)

The crew are small-time licensed hunters who take Ministry contracts and quietly keep what they find. Each early score teaches them that the contracts are not what they seem and that every Anchor belongs to a person. Meanwhile **the Understudied** (wearing **Elias Thorne**) is steering their reports and routing contracts to drive the **Subway Decay Clock**; **Apex Aegis** (with **Section Chief Kress** inside the Ministry) is skimming the bounty system and hunting for the Subway; and under everything, the **Omen** — the boundary thinning, something ancient waking — ticks forward on its own schedule. The **Architect** put the crew on this path for a reason that only makes sense once they care about someone. The campaign question: *does it matter why you started, if the work turned out to be real?*

## 4. Trigger Rules (If → Then)

These make the world move between sessions without railroading.

- **IF** the crew **warns Emmerich** (S2) → the Understudied's first attempt to silence him *failed*; it tries a second method (a Subway rumor, a Ministry flag, a "customer" at his shop) around **Session 4–6**. Emmerich will share the name only with earned trust — make the crew *protect* him before he talks.
- **IF** the crew **seizes the thimble** (S2) → Emmerich unravels; the one person who knew the true name is gone; Thorne becomes *warmer*, more helpful. Surface it later as "a convenient absence."
- **IF** the crew uses Double Bookkeeping and rolls 4–5 → tick **Ministry Audit** 1. **IF** Audit hits 4 → Vance sends a compliance team with four weeks' notice (the Vault and Cold Storage lock until a bribe).
- **IF** the crew protects a Twice-Born → +1 Favor *and* Kress notices them within ~2 sessions (paper trail: a rerouted contract).
- **IF** crew standing with Apex ≤ −2 or crew Heat 6+ → **Cleaners** assigned (they sabotage a score; they bicker about it).
- **IF** the crew goes to Harlan's → +1 intel lead ahead of the Ministry list (until Apex finds the diner).
- **IF** Decay reaches 4 → merchants pack up, Twice-Born flee (lose a Favor if holding 3), Apex intel triangulates.
- **IF** a PC reads the red-ink Journal entry (Long-Term Project) → Architect thread +1 clue; Harlan reacts ("Where'd you hear that name?").
- **IF** the crew carelessly leaves the Journal exposed → **Mara Halliday** stage 3.
- **IF** three incidents cluster in one district → Nesting clock (4-seg); see `Urban-Legends.md`.
- **IF** the crew has caught 3+ Thorne tells *and* told someone → the Understudied accelerates (it knows they know; it feeds a manufactured disaster).
- **IF** the Omen reaches 3 → AM Host fragments become *specific*.

## 6. The Endgame

**Three clocks converge:** the **Subway Decay** (6), the **Omen** (8) and the crew's **Ministry/Apex pressure** (Audit, Heat, Cleaners). The finale is a *choice*, not a boss fight.

### Act structure
| Act | Sessions | Question | Ends when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I — The Standard Work** | 0–3 | *What does this job cost?* | They have made three Anchor choices and entered the Subway |
| **II — The Squeeze** | 4–15 | *Who is lying to us?* | The Understudied is unmasked at the mid-season finale (S15): Scooby unmasking → real conversation |
| **III — The Cracks** | 16–77 | *What does the cover-up cost when it fails on its own?* | Kress falls or goes rogue (~S28); the Architect is named (~S54); Past the Yellow Line and Omen 7 (~S67) |
| **IV — The Waking** | 78–80 *(or earlier on "wrap it N")* | *What do we owe the thing we started?* | Omen 8 — **a train arrives at Line 0 Platform** *(the first train anyone boards; before this only a single pass-by is allowed — see `Living-Subway.md`)* |

### The Last Scene (default shape)
The **Line 0** departure board, which has not moved in the campaign, ticks. The **Architect** is revealed (per `The-Architect.md`) as the reason the crew was positioned *to be there*. The Omen event is not a monster — it is **the arrival of what the Subway was built for / hiding / waiting on** (depends on Mysteries A–D). The crew's decisions across the campaign answer it.

### Four End-State Families (pick by what the table has *done*)
| Ending | Triggered by | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **The Cover Holds** | Low Decay, high Ministry standing, Reyes empowered, Kress exposed | The cover-up survives *reformed* — registration for Twice-Born. A bittersweet win. The crew are licensed Ministry assets. |
| **The Cover Breaks** | Decay ≥ 6 | The supernatural is public. Chaos, then negotiation. The Understudied's plan, accidentally honored. The crew must choose whom to protect first. |
| **The Quiet Compact** | High Twice-Born Favor; Emmerich/Cosma/Mabel choices honored; the Understudied *turned or spared* | A negotiated peace — the Subway opens above ground, in part. Apex dismantled by the Ministry's reformers. |
| **The Subway Walks** | Decay 4–5 but Anchors honored; Omen answered | The Subway *leaves with everyone who wants to go.* The crew chooses between the city and the dark. |

### The Crew's Personal Endings
Each PC's **Heirloom** has a final stage (Full Degradation — see `Heirloom-Degradation-Patterns.md`). The Heirloom does not "pay off" as a prize; it **resolves what that character was carrying** in the last scene. Ask each player at ~S67 (Heirlooms at final stage), or in F2 of an express ending: *"What's in your pocket?"*

### Always-On Epilogue Questions (Anchor Ledger as the final scoreboard)
Read the Ledger aloud at the end: every name, every choice. *"These are the people you decided about."*

## 6b. Beyond Session 13 — the Campaign Is Open-Ended

**The original 13-session spine was the shortest path, not the length.** The campaign is now planned as 80 sessions in three seasons (see `Season-1-Plan.md`), with scores the players *choose* interleaved. Three ways to extend without losing the story:

1. **Side-score pool** *(interleave 1–2 between every spine session; no spine clock advances unless noted)*
   - `10-Generators/Urban-Legends.md` already contains **10 ready leads** (Glass Building, Fisherman's Report, Children's Game, Radio Signal, Empty Apartment, Dog Problem, Night Porter, Tailor's Referral, Archive Fire, Meridian Complaint).
   - **Rival-firm threads:** Harrow & Sons (alliance request / betrayal), Meridian Group (a contact learns Apex owns 40%), Castellan Retrieval (the hunter is Twice-Born; the handler doesn't know), Vantage & Lowe (rescue).
   - **Away Jobs** (see Appendix) — the Scooby episodes. *Run 2–3 of these across the campaign;* they're the comic breathers.
   - **Heirloom arcs:** each PC's Heirloom reaches stage 2 by ~S15, stage 3 by ~S41 and its final stage at ~S67; mid-arc beats are symptoms within a stage (a mini-session each).
   - **Mara Halliday's corruption** — a four-stage B-plot that can run for 10 sessions.
   - **The Wall of the Missing** at Harlan's: every missing hunter's name is a case.
2. **Fronts, not scripts.** Each faction advances *between sessions* by its "Moves on the Horizon" in `Faction-Tracker.md`. If the table slows the spine, the world speeds up: the Decay Clock and Omen tick on **fiction**, not on session counts.
3. **Sequel seeds (after the series finale).** Any of the four end states (§6) opens a sequel:
   - *Cover Holds* → the crew becomes **Ministry reformers** (and discovers why the Ministry was built).
   - *Cover Breaks* → **the first open year**; the crew are the only people who know how monsters *actually* work.
   - *Quiet Compact* → **the first embassy** between the Subway and the surface; the Understudied's rehabilitation.
   - *Subway Walks* → **the Road** (a long Away Job: following the Subway to wherever it relocates).

**Pace rule:** after every spine session, ask the table *"What do you want more of?"* — then pick the next side-score from that answer. The anchors hold by arc order, ±2 sessions.
