# Express Ending Kit

The table plays weekly for 2–3 hours and may need to end earlier than planned (≈ 6 months / ~26 sessions (sessions, not evenings; see `Night-Log.md`), 1 year / ~50, 1.5 years / ~75, or any time on short notice). This kit lets the GM wrap the **main story** in 3–4 sessions without it feeling rushed or abandoned. Everything here respects the single endgame in `Campaign-Arc.md` §6 and the 2026-11-20 canon (see `docs/review/Canon-Change-Log-2026-11-20.md`).

## 1. The command
GM says **"wrap it 2"** (F1 and F2 merged, then F3), **"wrap it 3"** or **"wrap it 4"** (the number of sessions left).
The Co-GM then:
1. Stops opening new mid arcs, episodes or Urban Legends.
2. Reads the **Living Finale Envelope** (§6) and tells the GM what it contains.
3. Declares **Arc IV begins early** — the first boardable train is now allowed.
4. Runs the **Compress Protocol** (§5) and preps the sequence below at normal Session 0–3 prep length.

## 2. The finale sequence (works from any state)
| Session | Title | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** | *Convergence* | The world speeds up: Omen to **7/8**; Subway Decay moves to match the crew's moral trajectory (see §5); Ministry/Apex pressure arrives at the shop's front door. One high-velocity score to seize the leverage that reaches Line 0 on the crew's terms. Cliffhanger: the Line 0 departure board moves. |
| **F2** | *The Reckoning* | (a) The Understudied: unmask now if not yet unmasked (Scooby `UNMASKED!` banner → silence → quiet conversation); if already unmasked, it returns with one last offer. (b) The Architect is revealed (GM's choice A–D, `03-NPCs/GM-Private/The-Architect.md`), paying off the red-ink journal entry, the lease and PTOLEMY's pre-dated ledger. (c) Each PC's Heirloom reaches its final stage — *"What's in your pocket?"* |
| **F3** | *The Waking* | Omen **8/8**. The Conductor's last announcement. The train arrives at Line 0 Platform and is boardable for the first and only time. The Final Choice, chosen from the state-to-ending table (§3), always with a human cost. For a 3-session wrap, end with the Anchor Ledger reading and one scene per PC. |
| **F4** *(wrap it 4)* | *The Morning After* | Read the **Anchor Ledger** aloud ("These are the people you decided about"). One playable scene per PC (~15 min each). The epilogue montage. Zoltar's last card. Consequence's last resting spot. |

## 3. State → ending
**How to read the table:** check the rows top to bottom; the first row whose conditions are all met wins. **If no row matches** (common when called early, e.g. at S12 or S26), take the row with the most conditions met and let F1's score target the missing one. If that still fails, default to **The Subway Walks**.

Check the live state at the start of F3 and offer the table **two real paths** (the primary and the alternative, each with a cost). Ending definitions are in `Campaign-Arc.md` §6.

| State at F3 | Primary ending | Alternative (with cost) |
|---|---|---|
| Decay ≤ 3, Reyes alive and empowered, Kress exposed, Ministry standing ≥ +1 | **The Cover Holds** | **The Quiet Compact** — cost: burn the crew's Ministry license to force the Subway embassy |
| Decay 6 (or pushed to 6 in F1–F2) | **The Cover Breaks** | **The Subway Walks** — cost: abandon the surface to save the Subway's people |
| Twice-Born Favor ≥ 2 (or most Anchors Honored/Returned) and the Understudied turned or spared | **The Quiet Compact** | **The Cover Holds** — cost: the Subway stays hidden another generation; the Understudied is imprisoned |
| Decay 4–5, Anchors mostly Honored, Ministry/Apex hostile | **The Subway Walks** | **The Cover Breaks** — cost: jam the Turnstiles open so nobody leaves quietly |

## 4. Built-in stop-points
- **~Session 26–28 (end of Season 1):** the Season 1 finale doubles as a complete ending. If the table knows by ~Session 24 it is stopping, replace the pass-by train with the boardable one and compress the Architect reveal into the last two sessions (Option B PTOLEMY is fastest; Option D Emmerich needs him alive and a Session 2 choice that kept him that way).
- **~Session 50–54 (end of Season 2):** the Architect reveal and Decay 4–5 / Omen 6 already stage the finale; `wrap it 3` rolls straight into Omen 8.
- **~Session 75–80 (end of Season 3):** the natural series finale; no compression.

## 5. Compress Protocol
1. **Clocks (the world speeds up).** Omen: 7/8 in F1, 8/8 in F3. Subway Decay keeps the crew's *moral* trajectory: if ≤ 2 it moves to 3; **3 stays 3**; 4 moves to 5; 5 stays 5; it reaches 6 only if the crew fails in F1. Never punish a careful crew with an unearned Decay 6. The Ministry Audit and Mara's corruption cash out as one live complication each in F1.
2. **Open threads → epilogue montage.** Unplayed Urban Legends, minor rival firms and unresolved arcs get two-sentence montage beats keyed to the chosen ending.
3. **NPC fates.** Every surviving canon NPC with two or more appearances gets one closing sentence tied to the crew's last interaction with them.
4. **Before the credits (non-negotiable).** Every PC gets one uninterrupted closing scene (2–5 min, player-directed): where they put their final-stage Heirloom, one person they visit or leave behind, and their answer to *"Does it matter why you started, if the work turned out to be real?"*

## 6. The Living Finale Envelope (15 minutes per season, and refresh it after every mid-arc climax)

3. **Leading ending, and the runner-up:** _______ / _______
4. **Each PC's final Heirloom scene** (one sentence each): _______
5. **The Convergence hook** — which active villain or ally starts F1 if the GM calls "wrap it" tomorrow: _______
