# Resonance-Flavored Complications

## [Category: Resonance Leak]
* **Scanner Ping:** An **[Faction: Apex Aegis]** Anchor Scanner pings within 200ft. 
* **Intercept:** A **[Faction: The Ministry]** shortwave intercept catches the signature; a bounty on the crew is drafted.
* **Owner Awareness:** The item's owner is suddenly aware of the theft and is walking toward the crew.
* **Slow Glass:** Every wristwatch and phone clock within 50 feet falls exactly eleven minutes behind real time, throwing off the crew's getaway window.
* **Sump Choir:** Every basement sump pump on the block kicks on at once in a rhythmic, wet metallic beat that drowns out whispered comms.
* **Static Recall:** A nearby car radio snaps to AM static and repeats the last embarrassed sentence one PC spoke during planning, loud enough for a guard to hear.
* **Salt Rim:** Fresh seawater rings appear around the soles of the crew's boots on dry pavement, leaving a track straight back to the bookmobile.
* **Cold Ink:** Every pen, printed form and fake ID in the crew's pockets turns blank white for ten minutes.

## [Category: Cover-Up Exposure]
* **The Witness:** A passerby has a 17-second video already uploading.
* **Ministry Liaison:** The liaison assigned to this district is **[Faction: The Mimic]**; the report will be compromised.
* **The Leak:** A child saw it and is telling a parent, who is calling the police.
* **Live Stream:** A neighborhood teenager is already streaming a vertical video titled *"Why are library people carrying rock salt at 2am?"* with 43 live viewers.
* **Form Audit:** Greta's pager buzzes: a Resonance sensor just opened an incident ticket, and she expects a three-page justification by 8am.
* **Rival Snap:** Someone in a Meridian Group windbreaker photographs the bookmobile's license plate and walks calmly around the corner.
* **Helpful Bystander:** An enthusiastic civilian believes the cover story completely and insists on escorting the crew right past the locked door they wanted to sneak through.
* **The Canteen Witness** *(from S2 on)*: Drennick and Foss turn the corner with takeout containers, pause mid-argument about pickled beets, and lock eyes with the worst-disguised PC.

## [Category: Subway-Adjacent]
* **Access Denied:** A **[Location: The Subway]** entrance stops letting the crew in without explanation.
* **PTOLEMY's Price:** **[NPC: PTOLEMY]** adds an entry to the ledger the crew doesn't remember agreeing to.
* **Broker's Nod:** **[NPC: Crossroads Broker]** is in the back booth, nodding at exactly one PC.
*(Use only after the crew has visited the Subway, from S2. The Subway has no fixed address and no train may be boarded before Arc IV.)*
* **Wrong Exit:** The Subway exit the crew expected won't open where they need it, and lets them out four blocks away in the Overhang.
* **PTOLEMY's Receipt:** A damp, brass-stamped receipt slips into a PC's coat pocket billing them for an "unscheduled Resonance overdraft" they haven't spent yet.
* **Dry Dust:** Fine, dry railway soot, impossible above this water table, drifts up through a storm grate at the crew's feet and draws a stray dog's stare.
* **The Broker's Counter-Offer** *(S13 and later)*: A folded diner napkin appears under Consequence the Cat with one handwritten line from the Crossroads Broker: *"You'll need a favor before Thursday."*
* **Passing Rumble** *(Omen 3+; never in the same session as a Subway visit)*: The floor trembles for six seconds with the vibration of a fast train passing deep under a district that has no subway. Nobody else notices.

## AI Facilitation Directives
* **Trigger Logic:** When a roll results in a complication, choose one item from a category that matches the current narrative stakes (e.g., use "Subway-Adjacent" if the crew is near an entrance).
* **Escalation:** If a **[Clock: Detection]** is at 3/4, prioritize "Cover-Up Exposure" complications.
