# Entanglement & Complication Cues

**S1 exception:** hold the S1 Entanglement until Greta is in the room (Step 5); never deliver it as her phone call before her first in-person scene.

**Rules for every cue:** the narration never says what the players do, say or feel; the NPC speaks only in 🗣 SAY; end each with ⏸ REACTION STOP; the funny line belongs to a person, never to a real threat (Menace Rule). Ministry rows may tick the Audit clock **silently** before S4 (`07-Clocks/Active-Clocks.md`). Entanglements are rolled after every score from S1.

## A. The twelve Entanglements (SRD names, our city)
Each: 🔊 READ ALOUD · 🗣 SAY · ❓ ASK · ⏸. Ways out are in `Heat-and-Entanglements.md` §2.

1. **Gang Trouble** *(your cohort's flaw)*
   🔊 "The bookmobile is idling outside the lair at six in the morning, and the horn will not stop. Somewhere inside, a very large dog is wagging."
   🗣 **SAY** *(Greta — flat, on the phone)*: "Form 47-B(ii) is not for noise complaints. Please remove the dog from the horn."
   ❓ ASK: "What do you do about it?" ⏸ REACTION STOP.
2. **The Usual Suspects** *(Ministry rounds up a friend)* 🔒 Audit +1, silent.
   🔊 "Greta's phone rings once and stops. When it rings again, the voice on it is someone else's. A contact of yours did not come to work this morning."
   🗣 **SAY** *(Greta — quiet)*: "They took someone who talks too much. I told them it wasn't you."
   ❓ ASK THE TABLE *(a player volunteers who)*: "Which of your contacts or purveyors is it?" ⏸. Then ❓ ASK: "Do you pay for them, or find out how much they will say?" ⏸.
3. **Questioning** *(a polite visit picks up an NPC)* 🔒 Audit +1.
   🔊 "Two people in grey stand by the canteen, each holding a clipboard. They are extremely polite. They ask the barista how to spell your contact's name."
   🗣 **SAY** *(a Ministry clerk — nervous)*: "Just a few routine questions. I'm sure it's nothing. It's always nothing."
   ❓ ASK: "How do you handle it?" ⏸.
4. **Interrogation** *(a PC is held)* 🔒 Audit +1. *Serious: no jokes.*
   🔊 "A bare room, one lamp, one chair that is bolted to the floor. The door closes without anyone touching it. The clock on the wall is wrong."
   🗣 **SAY** *(Ministry inquiry officer — mild, relentless)*: "Let us begin with where you were on the night of the incident. Slowly."
   ❓ ASK: "Pay, resist, or sit through it?" ⏸.
5. **Flipped** *(a contact bought by Apex)*
   🔊 "Your contact smiles when they see you. It is a good smile. It has been recently improved."
   🗣 **SAY** *(the flipped contact — warm, too warm)*: "I've been told to be friendly. I'm really glad you came."
   ❓ ASK: "What do you do about it?" ⏸. 🔒 They report to Apex Aegis; do not name it on screen yet.
6. **Rivals** *(another firm throws its weight around)*
   🔊 "A van with a polished crest pulls up across your usual spot and idles there, engine low, as if it were parked first."
   🗣 **SAY** *(Harrow & Sons junior, from the old family firm on the east side — bright)*: "Just checking this block's still yours. Heard it might be changing hands."
   ❓ ASK: "Stand your ground, or give something up?" ⏸.
7. **Reprisals** *(a friend or vice purveyor is squeezed)*
   🔊 "The corner shop where you buy coffee has a new sign in the window: CLOSED FOR INSPECTION. Inside, the owner stands very still behind the counter."
   🗣 **SAY** *(the shop owner — through the glass)*: "It's all right. They only wanted to talk."
   ❓ ASK: "What do you do about it?" ⏸.
8. **Unquiet Dead** *(an Echo from the last job)* *Menace Rule: it is scary.*
   🔊 "Wet footprints lead to your door and stop. The air in the hall smells of cold river clay."
   🗣 **SAY** *(a thin voice from the hall)*: "You took the thing. I want it back."
   ❓ ASK: "Open the door, or call someone?" ⏸. Use the Bestiary entry for the last job.
9. **Demonic Notice** *(something notices the crew)* 🔒 Never name the Omen or the Subway.
   🔊 "A card slides under the door: heavy cream stock, no name, one handwritten line. The ink is still wet and has no smell."
   🔊 *(show it as a `note` card and read it aloud)*: "We would like a word. At your convenience. Not yours, ours."
   ❓ ASK: "What do you do with it?" ⏸. Pull the offer from `Resonance-Complications.md`.
10. **Show of Force** *(Cleaners or a Ministry sweep)* 🔒 Ministry sweep: Audit +1, silent.
    🔊 "Three black vans park at both ends of the street. Nobody gets out. The streetlights go off one by one, in order."
    🗣 **SAY** *(a speaker — calm)*: "This is a courtesy. Please remain where you are for a courtesy."
    ❓ ASK: "Give up a claim (or let your lair come under pressure), or push back?" ⏸.
11. **Cooperation** *(an ally asks a favour)*
    🔊 "A kind face waits at the back of the room with their hat in their hands. They've been waiting a while."
    🗣 **SAY** *(the ally — apologetic)*: "I wouldn't ask if I had anyone else."
    ❓ ASK: "Do you say yes?" ⏸.
12. **Arrest** *(a Ministry detention)* 🔒 Audit +1. *No jokes.*
    🔊 "A door opens and closes behind a Ministry officer, who sets a form on the table and does not sit."
    🗣 **SAY** *(Greta — on the phone afterwards, quiet)*: "I can't get you out. I can tell you who to call."
    ❓ ASK: "Evade, pay, or hand someone over?" ⏸.

## C. Facilitation

- **One complication per bad roll.** Through S3 offer Resistance after every consequence (House-Rules §A); from S4 the players call for it.
- **Log it** (`Live-Ledger.md`): complication, clock changes, any Audit tick (silent before S4).
