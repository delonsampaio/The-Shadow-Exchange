# Bestiary — 40 One-Session Creatures

**Use:** pick by dread (Score-Catalog §E) and season. Build the prep file from the entry with `10-Generators/Three-Layer-Monster.md` and `00-Canon/Session-Writing-Rules.md`. **Menace Rule:** the creature is scary on screen; the jokes belong to the people (supers, plumbers, wardens, Greta). **Subway lines (Wrong exit, Cosma, etc.) only after the crew's first Subway visit (S2).**

**Source check (2026-11-20, Gemini):** all 40 Wikipedia links are live at the cited titles. For most entries the core folklore matches the page, but the **weaknesses, Anchors, rituals and local details are this table's inventions**, not folklore: do not present them to players as real tradition. Eight entries (#4, 13, 18, 27, 33, 36, 38, 40) are fully supported by the page. The modern-legend "real example" notes are in each entry's source.

**Respect rule for living traditions** (13 entries marked): treat the offering or custom as *working*; never make the spirit or the believers the joke; the antagonist is a human failure (a landlord, a developer, a company).

**Content rule (GM, 2026-11-20):** the table is very open: no content warnings. The one hard line: **nothing racist, and nothing that makes fun of a culture or a religion.** **Anything else goes:** gore, graphic violence, cursing, drowning, grief and fear are all fine. The non-gory versions in some entries (#2, #8) are only a default; use the traditional darker version whenever it suits the scene.

**Flags:** ⚠ marks a canon conflict or an adaptation to apply. Apply them before running.
