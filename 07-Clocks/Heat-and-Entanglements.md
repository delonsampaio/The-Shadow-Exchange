# Heat and Entanglements

## 1. Heat for a score (SRD)
Base by exposure: **0** smooth & quiet · **2** contained · **4** loud & chaotic · **6** wild. Then add **+1** high-profile or well-connected target · **+1** hostile turf (use it where the Apex scan vans or the Neighbourhood Watch patrol: the Overhang; Meridian scanners) · **+1** at war with a faction · **+2** if killing was involved (bodies draw attention). Track on the crew sheet. At **9**: **+1 Wanted level** and Heat clears (excess rolls over). The only way to lower Wanted (SRD) is incarceration: someone tied to the crew, or a framed enemy, is convicted, which gives Wanted −1 and clears Heat.

## 2. The Entanglement (rolled by the GM, SRD)
After payoff and Heat: roll **dice equal to Wanted level** and take the **highest** (if Wanted is 0, roll **two dice and keep the lowest**). Read the column for the crew's Heat: **0–3 / 4–5 / 6**.

| Result | Heat 0–3 | Heat 4–5 | Heat 6+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Gang Trouble or The Usual Suspects | Gang Trouble or Questioning | Flipped or Interrogation |
| 4–5 | Rivals or Unquiet Dead | Reprisals or Unquiet Dead | Demonic Notice or Show of Force |
| 6 | Cooperation | Show of Force | Arrest |

The Entanglement manifests before the crew can avoid it; bring it in at once or save it for the right moment (the vice scene, the Ministry office). Give each one a **cover-up face** before an injury:

| SRD entanglement | In this city | Ways out (SRD) |
|---|---|---|
| Gang Trouble | **Your own cohort's flaw** causes trouble (Gerald makes a scene) | Lose face (−rep equal to Tier + 1), make an example of the offender, or face reprisals from the wronged party |
| The Usual Suspects | Greta's phone: the Ministry rounds up the usual suspects and takes a friend or contact | Fortune roll for how much they talk (1–3: +2 Heat; 4/5: level 2 harm), or pay 1 Coin |
| Questioning | A polite Ministry or Apex visit with a clipboard picks up an NPC contact (Drennick and Foss, once met) | Fortune roll (1–3: +2 Heat; 4/5: +1 Heat), or pay 2 Coin |
| Interrogation | A PC is held for a Ministry "inquiry" | Pay 3 Coin, **or** level 2 harm and +3 Heat (each resistable) |
| Flipped | A contact or patron is bought by Apex (a Stalls-type shell) and starts reporting | They are now loyal to another faction |
| Rivals | Another firm (Harrow & Sons, Meridian Group, Castellan) throws its weight around | Forfeit 1 rep or 1 Coin per Tier of the rival, or stand up to them and lose 1 status |
| Reprisals | A friend, contact or vice purveyor is squeezed by the Ministry or Apex | Pay 1 rep + 1 Coin per Tier of the enemy, let them mess with you, or fight back |
| Unquiet Dead | An Echo or ghost tied to the last job comes looking (`Three-Layer-Monster.md`; Menace Rule: it is scary) | Hire an occultist (Acquire Asset; roll its quality) or deal with it yourselves |
| Demonic Notice | **Something notices** (the Omen, or the Subway through PTOLEMY's ledger): a quiet, strange offer or complication from `Resonance-Complications.md` | Accept its bargain, hide until it loses interest (forfeit 3 rep), or deal with it another way |
| Show of Force | Apex Cleaners or a Ministry sweep makes a visible point | Give up 1 claim, or go to war (−3 status); with no claims, lose 1 hold |
| Cooperation | An ally at +3 status (a Twice-Born, the Hunter Underground) asks a favour | Do it, or forfeit 1 rep per Tier or lose 1 status; with no +3 faction, no entanglement |
| Arrest | A Ministry detention: a detail at least equal to your Wanted in scale | Pay Coin equal to Wanted + 3, **hand someone over** (this clears Heat), or evade. Wanted −1 only if that person is *convicted* (SRD) |

**Pick the row that fits the fiction first; roll when nothing suggests itself.** Entanglements stay a cost of doing business, not a punishment: use the ways out above and move on unless the table wants to play it.
