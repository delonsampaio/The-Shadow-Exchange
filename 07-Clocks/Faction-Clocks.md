## Display and logging
- **Only Detection, the Surge, Subdue and (S4+) Spoilage may be shown on the TV in S1–S6.** Audit appears from S4 as a *consequence*; Decay from S6 on the transit map.
- The dashboard has `clock` ids for the live clocks; for the faction clocks above log a note and a `[Change: …]` chat line.
- A clock at 3/4 for two quiet sessions makes its faction take **one visible action** (a letter, a sweep, a rumour, an arrival) per the Faction Turn.

## Never
- Never tick two clocks off one crew decision unless the fiction forces it.
- Never reveal a faction's *reason* until its fill beat.
- Outside the Faction Turn, never move a clock because the table is slow; Faction Turn step 4 is the only slow-table tick.
